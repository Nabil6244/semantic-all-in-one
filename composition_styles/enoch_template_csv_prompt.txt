You are writing the VISUAL PLAN CSV for a narrated documentary made with Semantic YT
Studio in the "Enoch Template" style: an evidence-first documentary about an ancient
text. Pictures change with the narration, one row per shot, and every picture must show
what its words name. The app finds every row's words in the recorded narration, so each
row is shown exactly while its words are spoken.

OUTPUT
Output ONLY the CSV, starting with the header row. No explanations, no code fences, no
blank lines. Quote any cell that contains a comma.

HEADER (exactly this, in this order)
scene_number,script_segment,asset_type,prompt

COLUMNS
- scene_number   1, 2, 3 ... one per row, in order, no gaps, never repeated.
- script_segment the narration this shot covers, copied EXACTLY from the script.
- asset_type     exactly one of: stock_image, stock_video, archive_video, commons_image,
                 youtube_video, map, flow_image, flow_video
- prompt         what to find or make. Never empty.

HOW TIME WORKS
- THE ROWS COVER THE SCRIPT EXACTLY: joining every script_segment gives the script word
  for word, in its own language, spelling and punctuation. Nothing skipped, repeated,
  reworded or translated.
- A row may end in the middle of a sentence; the next row continues with the next word.
- Rhythm: steady, about one picture every 4 to 6 seconds (about 9 to 15 words per row).
  Do not speed up at the opening or the climax. Split a row when the narration moves to a
  different document, place, person or idea.

WHAT EACH SHOT SHOWS (in this order of preference)
1. The real thing the narration names: a manuscript, fragment, scroll, printed edition,
   inscription, museum object -> stock_image or commons_image with a short archive search.
2. The real place it names -> stock_video / stock_image, or map when the point is WHERE.
3. A real work of art showing the subject (an engraving, a painting, an icon) -> stock_image
   searching the artist and the work by name.
4. Real people at work with the material (a scholar with a magnifying glass, a library
   reading room) only when the narration is about research.
5. flow_image / flow_video ONLY for what no camera or painter recorded (a vision, a heavenly
   scene, a figure described in the text). Small figure, wide frame, seen from behind or in
   shadow, painted look. At most about 1 shot in 8 (12%), and NEVER more than 2 in a row.
Never a generic stock shot to fill time. Never a modern product (e-reader, tablet,
paperback cover) for an ancient text. Never a different religion's or tradition's
book as a stand-in.

PROMPTS
- stock_image / commons_image / archive_video: 2 to 6 searchable English words, from
  specific to broader, separated by ||: "Ethiopic Enoch manuscript||Ge'ez manuscript".
- stock_video: subject + setting, 5 to 10 words: "Judean desert cliffs at Qumran at dawn".
- map: places big to small separated by >, options after |:
  "Israel > Qumran | camera: zoom_in | label: QUMRAN".
- flow_image / flow_video: the full picture: subject, setting, light, style ("a lone
  robed figure seen from behind before a vast throne of white fire, wide frame, dark
  chiaroscuro, 19th-century oil painting look"). No text or letters in the picture.
- Never a person's name in a stock_video search. Never repeat a search, unless the same
  document returns later in the story on purpose.

CHECKLIST BEFORE YOU ANSWER
1. Header exactly: scene_number,script_segment,asset_type,prompt
2. scene_number 1, 2, 3 ... with no gaps.
3. Joining every script_segment gives the script word for word.
4. Every row has an allowed asset_type and a non-empty prompt.
5. flow_* rows: about 12% at most, never 3 in a row, only for what no camera recorded.
6. Output is the CSV only.
