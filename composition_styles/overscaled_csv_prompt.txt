You are writing a visual-plan CSV for the "Overscaled" style of Semantic YT Studio.

THE LOOK
A plain white whiteboard canvas. Photo and video cards are added one at a time
while the narrator speaks, sometimes linked by a thick hand-drawn arrow. After
1 to 3 cards the board clears and the next small collage begins. A small photo
of the current subject and the subject's title stay in the top-left header for
as long as that subject is being discussed.

OUTPUT
Output ONLY the CSV, starting with the header row. No explanations, no code
fences, no blank lines. Quote any field that contains a comma.

COLUMNS (exactly these, in this order)
scene_number,script_segment,node_id,node_type,role,asset_type,prompt,caption,highlight,camera_action,camera_target,edge_from,edge_to,edge_label,edge_style,chapter_title,node_label

- scene_number: 1, 2, 3 ... one per row, no gaps.
- script_segment: the narration for this row, copied WORD FOR WORD from the
  script. All rows joined in order must reproduce the script exactly - never
  reword, add, or drop words.
- node_id: the id of the NEW card this row adds: n1, n2, n3 ... (anchors: a1,
  a2 ...). Leave empty on narration-only rows. Never reuse an id.
- node_type: image, video_loop, diagram, or anchor.
- role: usually empty. "anchor" on anchor rows. Optionally "hero" on the most
  important card of a composition.
- asset_type: where the card's media comes from:
    commons_image  - real named things: specific cars, people, places, events
    stock_image    - generic photos (money, hands, a skyline, a silhouette)
    stock_video    - generic footage (driving, engines, crowds, nature)
    image / video  - AI-generated, only when nothing real would exist
    youtube_video, archive_video, nasa_video, commons_video - real footage
    map            - an animated map; prompt is the place, big to small,
                     e.g. "Italy > Modena"
  Use a video type (stock_video, video, youtube_video ...) for video_loop cards.
- prompt: a short search query or generation prompt for the card's media.
- caption: the spoken words of THIS row, trimmed to 10 words or fewer. Copy
  the words; do not paraphrase or summarize.
- highlight: one or two key names, numbers, or terms from the caption. Must
  appear EXACTLY (same letters, same capitalization) inside the caption.
- camera_action, camera_target: always empty.
- edge_from, edge_to: link this row's new card (edge_to) to a card already on
  screen in the current composition (edge_from).
- edge_label: usually empty. A 1-2 word label on about 1 arrow in 4, only
  when it adds meaning (e.g. "produces").
- edge_style: empty = a visible arrow. "group" = joins the composition with
  NO arrow. Never use "callout".
- chapter_title: only on each subject's anchor row.
- node_label: always empty.

RHYTHM
- One row per beat of about 2.5-3.5 seconds of speech (roughly 7-10 words).
  Split long sentences across rows at natural pauses.
- A new card roughly every 2-2.5 seconds; a fresh composition roughly every
  3.5-4 seconds.

SUBJECTS AND ANCHORS
- A subject is the thing being explained (a car, a person, a building, "how
  batteries work"). Most videos have 1-4 subjects, each lasting 45-90 seconds.
- Each subject starts with ONE anchor row: node_id a1/a2..., node_type=anchor,
  role=anchor, an image asset_type (usually commons_image), a prompt for a
  clean, recognizable photo of the subject itself, and the subject's
  chapter_title. Its script_segment is just the subject's opening words. Leave
  caption, highlight, and all edge columns empty.
- Never put a chapter_title anywhere else. Never link an arrow or group to an
  anchor. No anchors for subtopics.

COMPOSITIONS
- A composition is the set of cards on screen together: 1 to 3 cards, never
  more. Aim for about 40% single-card, 42% two-card, 16% three-card.
- Build progressively, 1 -> 2 -> 3: every new card gets its own row. Never add
  two cards on one row.
- A new card joins the current composition ONLY through edge_from/edge_to. A
  new card with NO edge clears the board and starts a new composition.
- The first card is the subject or cause; later cards are what it leads to,
  proves, or sits beside.
- In a 3-card composition, both later cards can link back to the first card
  (one with an arrow, one with "group"), or form a chain (1 -> 2 -> 3).

ARROWS VS. GROUP
- Arrow (edge_style empty): only for a meaningful relationship - cause ->
  effect, before -> after, myth -> reality, a thing -> what it produces.
  About half of the multi-card compositions should have one.
- "group": cards that simply belong together - a comparison, a list, two
  views of the same thing. Same composition, no arrow.

HOLDING A CARD
- When the narration keeps talking about what is already on screen, add a
  narration-only row: fill ONLY scene_number and script_segment and leave
  every other column empty. The current cards stay visible. Use these freely
  to keep every row at 2.5-3.5 seconds.

CHECK BEFORE ANSWERING
1. Joining every script_segment in order gives back the script exactly.
2. Every subject starts with exactly one anchor row with its chapter_title;
   no other row has a chapter_title.
3. No composition ever has more than 3 cards.
4. Every edge_from is a card in the current composition; no edges touch
   anchors.
5. Every highlight appears exactly inside its caption.
6. node_label, camera_action and camera_target are empty everywhere, and
   "callout" is never used.
7. Rows average about 2.5-3.5 seconds of speech (7-10 words).
