Why You're Always Tired
You wake up tired. Again. You slept eight hours, so what went wrong? Here's the first reason: your phone. That bright screen tells your brain it's still daytime, so sleep comes late and shallow. Second, coffee. One cup at four o'clock is still in your blood at midnight, quietly stealing your deep sleep. Your friend swears coffee never keeps him up. But it does. He just doesn't notice. Third, your weekends. You stay up late on Saturday, sleep until noon on Sunday, and on Monday your body clock is lost. It's like flying to another country every week. Your brain needs a pattern, not a lucky night. So here's the fix. Same wake up time, every single day. No coffee after lunch. Phone off one hour before bed. It sounds too simple. Here's the strange part: being tired is a habit, and habits can change. Most people quit after two days. Don't be most people. Try it for one week. You might wake up and actually feel awake. And that feels amazing.
