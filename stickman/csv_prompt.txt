STICKMAN CSV — write it from my script

You are writing a Stickman CSV for the Stickman style of Semantic YT Studio. Two stick figures (the LEAD, who is "you"
in the script, and a FRIEND who walks in the first time the script talks about someone else) act out every row while
the narrator speaks it. Read my script, then return ONE CSV file and nothing else.

COLUMNS (this header, this order):
line,action,props,place,text,camera,sfx,look

RULES
1. line — the narration, word for word. Put the rows together and they must be EXACTLY my script (same words, same
   order; punctuation may stay). One short spoken line per row: 3 to 8 words, ending at a natural pause (a full stop,
   a comma, a clause). Long sentences take several rows. The title line is not narration: leave it out.
2. Every other cell may be left EMPTY: the director then picks from the words itself. Fill a cell only where you
   want something specific. Aim to fill action on about half the rows, props on about a third, place only when the
   scene really moves somewhere, text on 3 to 6 rows (numbers, key ideas), camera and sfx rarely.
3. action — one pose. For the friend write friend:pose (e.g. friend:shrug). Poses:
   stand point shrug think cheer facepalm scared wave clap dance laugh cry sad angry tired yawn arms_crossed
   hands_hips present stop look phone type carry push pull lift sit sleep fall kneel crouch bow fist_up idea shiver
   walk run sneak jump
   The first row is always the lead running in (leave its action empty). Don't give the same pose to the same
   figure three rows in a row.
4. props — one or two, comma separated, optionally @where (side, above, ground, hand_f, hands, face):
   phone laptop tv camera battery_full battery_low wifi rocket clock alarm hourglass calendar moon sun star zzz bulb
   brain heart question exclaim check cross speech thought arrow arrow_up arrow_down chart_up chart_down target flag
   trophy medal money coin moneybag gift key lock house bed pillow chair desk lamp plant window door frame fridge
   counter cup burger apple pill drop book pencil magnifier box barbell bomb car bike plane globe tree cloud rain bolt
   fire mountain building streetlamp bench board
5. place — bedroom kitchen office street park city_night classroom void. Keep a place for at least 4 rows.
6. text — a big word card at the top while the row is spoken, at most 24 characters ("8 HOURS", "RULE #1").
7. camera — wide, medium or close. sfx — pop whoosh boing sparkle burst chime success drumroll ding_1 … ding_10.
8. look — first row only, optional: ink_paper chalk_dark colour_pop retro_tv night_thriller meme. Add +host for my
   picture as a presenter in the corner (e.g. meme+host). Only if I ask for them.
9. Quote any cell that contains a comma. No extra columns, no notes, no blank rows.

EXAMPLE
line,action,props,place,text,camera,sfx,look
You wake up tired.,,,bedroom,,,,colour_pop
Again.,facepalm,,,,close,,
"You slept eight hours,",sleep,clock@side,,8 HOURS,,,
