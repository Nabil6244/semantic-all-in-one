## The three formats that work
**A. Voiced quiz in rounds** (Recall Lane, Still Sharp, Quiz Magnet 1950s): the main format.
- **Intro (12–22 s):** the challenge and pass mark ("Score 35 or more and you pass"), "getting harder as we go", "you only get 6 seconds each", "grab a pen, keep score", and a tease for a **bonus question at the end**.
- **3–5 named rounds** in rising difficulty, with topic titles ("Round one, grandma's gadgets", "Round three, the hard round"). Quiz Magnet names them by level: warm-up, easy, intermediate, difficult, expert.
- **Each question takes about 28 s:** the voice reads the question and all the options (A, B, C, D) for 10–12 s, then a **6–7 s silent countdown** under music, then "The answer is D, Pennsylvania" plus **one fun fact** (6–8 s).
- **Score check after each round:** "End of round one. 12 or more? You really…", "28 or more so far?"
- **End:** the bonus question, then a score ladder ("under 20, no worries, there's always room for more pie"), "tell me your score in the comments" (plus "which question brought back the most memories"), "subscribe for a new quiz every day", and "the quiz on your screen is waiting for you" over an end screen.
- **Math:** 50 questions × 28 s ≈ 24 min. 30 questions ≈ 13 min.

**B. Silent picture ID** (Quiz Magnet "50 Old Items", 1.2M views, the biggest hit):
- The voice speaks only in the intro and outro. Everything else is music plus a ticking sound.
- **Each item:** a large, realistic photo on a plain light background, the item number in the corner, and a **time bar that drains from green to red** over 7 s. Then the item's name appears in a bar along the bottom. About 10 s per item, 50 items in under 10 min.
- It ends with a **"bonus mystery object"**: "tell me in the comments along with your score out of 50".

**C. Silent brain test** (Knowledge Dose / Knowledge Engine, "Are you around 72?"):
- A 25-minute run of 40+ general-knowledge questions. No voice after the 10 s intro: music, a bell on each reveal, and a ticking clock.
- **Layout:** a numbered question box, 3 options (a/b/c), a countdown circle (about 10 s), then the right answer as a large highlighted pill.
- The channel uploads near-identical videos every day with only the age changed in the title (65, 66, 67…).

## Look (from the frames)
- **Recall Lane (closest to what our app does best):**
  - warm cream "paper" background;
  - a **painted vintage Americana picture** on the left (Flow-like oil/gouache style), with the question in a serif font and the options on the right;
  - a countdown number over the picture and a red progress line under it;
  - the reveal: the right option turns green with a tick, and a dark caption strip shows the fact.
- **Still Sharp:** a dark olive gradient with no pictures, a 2×2 grid of options, a numbered countdown circle, the right answer in green with a tick, and a "Did you know" box.
- **All of them:** very large text, at most 4 options, the question number and round always on screen, and one calm music bed for the whole video.

## Thumbnails
- An **age or year hook:** "BORN BEFORE 1955?", "Are you around 72?", "Remember these old objects?"
- A **score challenge:** "Your brain is young if you score 4 out of 10!"
- A **count badge:** "50 CLUES · 50 STATES", "QUIZ · 30 QUESTIONS".
- One strong nostalgic object (a percolator) or a warm, friendly face of someone aged 60–75. Bold, high contrast, 3–5 words.

## Sound
One soft music bed for the whole video, a ticking clock during the countdown, a bell or chime on the reveal. Voices are calm and plain, with no hype.

## What we take into our style
1. Use **format A** as the main mode, with our painted Flow pictures (the Recall Lane approach): round-based voiced questions with a pass mark and a bonus question at the end.
2. Use **format B as a second mode:** "Name these 50 old items", silent apart from the intro and outro. It's the cheapest to make and the biggest hit.
3. Score checks after each round, a score ladder at the end, and a comment prompt ("your score + which brought back memories").
4. **Timing:** 6–7 s countdown, about 28 s per question, 30–50 questions, ending with a bonus question.
5. **Daily series titles:** age and year hooks with variations (Born before 1955 / 1960; Are you around 70 / 72).
6. **Our additions:** the **host** in 5–6 talking clips, our own colours and layout, never a still screen (slow push-in on pictures, a moving timer), and pictures in the reveal.
