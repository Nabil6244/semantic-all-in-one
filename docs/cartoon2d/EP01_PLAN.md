# Episode 1 — "Colors with Miss Molly & Bobo the Bear" — plan

Cast: **Miss Molly** (host), **Lily** (girl), **Ollie** (cheeky little brother), **Bobo the Bear** (funny sidekick, placeholder name). Channel name still open. Length: **18–20 min**.
Spelling and voices: **US English** ("color"); the largest preschool audience searches in US English.

## 1. What grabs and holds a preschooler's attention (research, 2026-11-15)

| Finding | Source | Rule for us |
|---|---|---|
| Songs and nursery rhymes dominate the most-viewed kids videos (Baby Shark is YouTube's most-viewed video; Cocomelon has two in the all-time top 10). | Digital Trends, KidScreen | Every episode has a sing-along / chant. |
| Children watch what they **understand**; comprehension drives attention (Anderson). Formal features (cuts, movement, sound effects, **child voices**) draw attention; adult male voices and long zooms lose it. | Kirkorian, Wartella & Anderson; SAGE Encyclopedia of Children and Media | One clear idea at a time; kids' voices often; a sound on every event; constant character movement; no long zooms. |
| Blue's Clues: a question, then a ~2 s pause, plus repetition, raised answering, pointing and comprehension; minimal editing kept focus. | Crawley & Anderson study; Disney Research | Ask → pause 1.6–2 s → answer → praise; repeat each key word 3+ times; recap quiz at the end. |
| Cocomelon tests prototypes against a "Distractatron" and cuts every ~1–3 s; the AAP criticises that pace as too fast. | NYT via secondary sources; AAP | Lively but not frantic: a new shot or new picture every **4–6 s**, never a still screen. |
| Autoplay means a few seconds to hook. | AIR, Netinfluencer (Kidscreen Summit) | Cold open: a character, a question and a surprise inside **5 s**; no logo intro; title card ≤ 2.5 s. |
| Thumbnails: one big expressive face, one object, high contrast, a short word in a round font; test 2–3 versions in YouTube Studio. | YouTube Help; vidIQ | Thumbnail: Ollie surprised + red balloon + "COLORS!"; 3 variants. |
| Quality rules (YouTube kids principles, inauthentic-content policy). | YouTube Help; Tubefilter | Real learning goal, kind behaviour, no brands, every episode new. |

## 2. Episode 1 at a glance

- **Length:** 18–20 min (16:9), built from 11 segments of 1–3 min joined by one story, so a toddler can drop in anywhere and still follow. Shorts and a 2–4 min "color song" clip are cut from it later.
- **Cast:** Miss Molly, Lily, Ollie, and a new funny character, **Bobo the Bear** (placeholder name; the user's "bhalu" idea).
- **Goal:** red, blue and yellow, named and found in the world, repeated across the whole episode.
- **Places:** Rainbow Playroom → the Garden (new) → back to the Playroom for the song, dance and goodbye. A place change about every 6–8 min keeps it fresh.
- **Spine:** Bobo has lost his three colored balloons (red, blue, yellow); the friends find one color in each segment and he gets them all back at the end.

## 3. Bobo the Bear

| | |
|---|---|
| Look | Round honey-brown bear, cream tummy, small red bow tie; same flat cartoon style and outlines as the others. |
| Personality | Big, gentle, goofy and clumsy; gets colors wrong on purpose so the child can correct him; never mean, never scary. |
| Running gags | Tumbles in (falls into the ball pit); says the wrong color ("Is a banana red?" → "Nooo! Yellow!"); sneezes "a-a-achoo!" which makes the kids laugh; loves honey. |
| Voice | Warm, silly, a little low but bouncy (an Edge voice with pitch and rate tuned); no deep adult-news voice. |
| Catchphrase | "Oopsie-daisy, silly Bobo!" |

## 4. Segment plan (about 19 min)

| # | Time | Segment | What happens | Interaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 | **Cold open** | Ollie runs in with a red balloon: "Look what I found!" → Bobo tumbles into the ball pit, balls fly: "Oopsie-daisy!" | hook in 5 s; title card 2.5 s |
| 2 | 0:40 | **Hello song** | Molly, Lily, Ollie and Bobo sing a hello chant and wave; Bobo explains he lost his balloons | wave with us |
| 3 | 2:30 | **RED hunt** (Playroom) | apple, strawberry, fire truck, Ollie's red star; Bobo: "Is a banana red?" | ask → pause → answer, 3+ "red" |
| 4 | 5:00 | **Red balloon found** | Ollie has it; sharing moment: he gives it back to Bobo | kindness |
| 5 | 6:00 | **Off to the Garden** | everyone marches out ("march, march, march!") | movement break |
| 6 | 7:00 | **BLUE hunt** (Garden) | blue sky, blue bird, blue ball, Lily's blue dress, Molly's overalls; Bobo: "Is the grass blue?" | pauses, corrections |
| 7 | 9:30 | **YELLOW hunt** (Garden) | sun, duck, banana, lemon, Ollie's shirt; Bobo sneezes and the yellow balloon floats down from a tree | pauses, corrections |
| 8 | 12:00 | **Bobo's silly paint** | back in the Playroom, Bobo paints a blue banana and a purple sun; the kids fix every picture | "That's silly! What color should it be?" |
| 9 | 14:30 | **Color song** | clap-along chant on the beat; cards flash on each color word | clap with us |
| 10 | 16:00 | **Color freeze dance** | dance until Molly shows a color, then freeze; Bobo can't freeze and wobbles | stand up and play |
| 11 | 17:30 | **Quiz + goodbye** | quick picture quiz of all three colors; goodbye song; "next time we'll count with Bobo!" | answer, wave |

Script: about 220–260 lines (~1,800 words), written segment by segment against the checklist in CONTENT_PLAN.md; every question has a 1.6–2 s pause and every color is said 3+ times per segment.

## 6. Engine work for this episode

1. **Bobo's rig:** the same cutting tool, with fur in place of sleeves, and a 4-character layout and cameras.
2. **Hold a prop and hand it over:** a balloon fixed to a hand, bobbing on its string, passed from one character to another.
3. **Funny moves:** tumble-in (spin + bounce + ball-pit splash), sneeze (wind-up, then a burst), wobble when he can't freeze, a march, and a dance.
4. **Place changes:** a wipe transition between Playroom and Garden, each place with its own floor line and character spots.
5. **Close-up shots** per character, used for the hook, jokes and reactions.
6. **Songs:** a hello song, a color song and a goodbye song spoken on the beat (`kids_audio/chant.py`), with everyone clapping or bouncing on the beat.
7. **Lines spoken together** (two or four characters at once).
8. **Title and end cards** with a jingle; color word cards in their own colors.
9. **Long render:** 20 min ≈ 30,000 frames, about 40–60 min in the background, once.
10. **Thumbnail maker:** 3 versions.

## 7. Checks before the one final render

- Stills sheet of every segment: faces clear, no card on a face, props in hands, nobody cut by the frame edge, Bobo never scary.
- Script rules: one idea at a time, a pause after every question, colors repeated, kind behaviour, Bobo's mistakes always corrected.
- Pace: no shot longer than ~6 s without new movement or a new picture; a song or movement break at least every 4 min.
- Audio: voices clearly over music, -14 LUFS, an effect on every card and joke.

## 8. Publishing package

- **Title:** "Learn Colors with Miss Molly & Bobo the Bear 🎈 Red, Blue, Yellow | Toddler Learning Video"
