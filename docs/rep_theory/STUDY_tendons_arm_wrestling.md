## 2. How a Flow picture becomes "video"

Each technique below was read from frames 0.3-0.5 s apart.

1. **Full-bleed, not a framed inset.** The picture fills the whole 16:9 frame. The ink drawing sits on pure black, and the
   text goes in the empty black part (usually right, sometimes top-left). We paste our pictures as a small panel on the left.
2. **A strong camera move on every shot, toward what is being said.** 0:00-0:04: the face-off pushes in about 35% and slides
   toward the big guy's face as the narration says "way bigger than you". The "BIGGER" leader label appears on his arm
   at that moment. Our push is 6-10%, centred on nothing.
3. **Re-framing the same picture = a new shot.** The seated test picture is shown wide (0:56 "quick test"), then cropped
   close to the forearm on the table (1:00 "rest your forearm"), then close on the hand edge ("hand off"). The same goes
   for the towel pull (6 shots from 2 pictures: wide, elbow close-up, chest-height close-up), the hammer (wide, mid, the
   hand), the bucket (hand close-up, side view, the squeeze crop) and the "line" posture. One picture covers 10-25 s
   without ever looking held.
4. **Red paint grows on the ink drawing.** Pictures are greyscale ink with one accent. The muscle or tendon being talked
   about is painted glowing red, and the red **grows in on the word**: along the forearm from elbow to fingers (0:08-0:11,
   with a red arrow sliding the same way: "force reaches your fingers"), on the hand gripping the rope ("stiff tendons"),
   and the tendon lines in the bucket hand ("the longest tendons"). The picture is drawn with the red. The edit
   reveals it.
5. **A second picture of the same moment for the punchline.** "the guy with the giant bicep just folds": cut from the
   even face-off to a version where the big guy is losing, with speed lines and a slammed "FOLDS" stamp. Before/after
   pairs of one scene.
6. **Overlays on the picture, each on its word.** Leader-line labels (BIGGER, GIANT BICEP, FOREARM, SHOULDER/BICEP,
   FOREARM/WRIST/FINGERTIPS), a target ring on the joint being named, force arrows (curl up, pull sideways), a dashed line
   for the table edge, a measuring bar ("LONGEST"), a green alignment line ("stand tall"), a ghost duplicate drifting
   sideways ("don't let it pull you sideways"), a desaturated, dimmed copy of the figure ("rest"), and the stopwatch top right.
7. **Handwritten note + drawn arrow** on the hook ("not muscle" with a red arrow), the only handwriting in the video, on
   the most important claim. It comes back in the last minute with one more label ("TRAIN THE END").
8. **Focus-pull and blur between shots**, a glitch on the "QUICK TEST" title as it leaves, and a red speed-line burst on stamps.

## 4. Rules this adds to the tool (all general, none tied to this script)

1. **Never a text-only screen.** Every sentence gets a picture or a diagram. A bare headline is a fallback only when
   neither exists, and the quality check counts it.
2. **The opening shot is the hook picture**: the promise of the video in one image (the two sides of a contrast, the
   person doing the thing), full frame, with a push-in.
3. **Pictures are full frame with a real camera move**: wide → push 20-35% toward the subject, and the next sentence on the
   same picture gets a close framing on another part (re-framing instead of a new picture or a repeat).
4. **One picture per idea, 2-4 framings per picture.** Consecutive sentences about the same moment share a picture; a new
   moment gets a new picture. No upper limit on pictures. The cap was the main reason our video looked empty.
5. **Red highlight reveal**: pictures are ink drawings with the muscle or tendon in glowing red. The red is hidden at the start
   of the shot and grows in on the word that names it.
6. **Mechanism → diagram, list → filling slots, number → counter, study → study card, analogy → literal drawing, plan →
   building list, test → stopwatch, payoff → lock that opens later, ending → callback.** Most of these cards already exist
   in `cards.py`. The new ones are listed in section 5.
7. **The 4-part tracker**: when the script announces N parts, the slots become a persistent tracker that opens each unit
   and gets ticks. That is already partly built (PART n/N, NEXT teasers); it needs the slot visual.
8. **Text sits in the picture's empty side** in two sizes (small white kicker + big red/orange/green headline), never as
   its own screen. Words on screen can be a short formula built from the sentence ("BIG BICEP ≠ SAFE", "STIFF = FAST").
