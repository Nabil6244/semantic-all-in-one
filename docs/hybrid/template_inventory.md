## Automatic placement rules

### chapter_serif
- requires: 'Part/Chapter N' and a title of 2-7 words in the same or the next sentence
- eligible when: a titled section is announced
- where: map, footage
- placement: serif kicker over a tracked title
- timing: from the announcement to the end of the title
- fallback: no title = chapter_words

### counter_corner
- requires: the same quantity as big_number
- eligible when: a quantity, when the middle of the picture should stay clear
- where: map, footage
- placement: corner panel, top right
- timing: from the sentence start
- fallback: a temperature is left to big_number

### keyword_title
- requires: a punchline (below) of at most five words
- eligible when: a short sentence lands as a beat
- where: map, footage
- placement: heavy words one by one, the longest boxed in red
- timing: from the sentence start
- fallback: six or more words = a text style instead

### lower_kicker
- requires: a person with what they are, as for name_bar
- eligible when: the narrator introduces a person
- where: map, footage
- placement: lower third with a kicker (the role) and the name
- timing: from the name
- fallback: no role = nothing (name_bar needs one too)

### record_badge
- requires: what record, where, new value and date, old value and date, all in one sentence
- eligible when: a broken record is stated in full
- where: map, footage
- placement: badge
- timing: from the record words
- fallback: anything missing = no badge

### stat_countup
- requires: the same quantity as big_number
- eligible when: a quantity, when another number layout was just shown
- where: map, footage
- placement: white number counting up, label typed beside it (dims the frame)
- timing: from the sentence start
- fallback: a temperature is left to big_number

### quote_card
- requires: a quotation in quote marks of 4-30 words, a speaker named in the sentence or 8+ words
- eligible when: the sentence quotes someone
- where: map, footage
- placement: left block, soft backing over footage
- timing: from the first quoted word to the last, +1 s
- fallback: a quotation is never shortened: too long or too short = no card

### end_question
- requires: one of the narration's last two sentences, a question of 3-14 words
- eligible when: the narrator closes with a question
- where: map, footage
- placement: centre, words popping in on a dimmed frame (backing over footage)
- timing: from the question's first word
- fallback: no question = nothing

### fact_card
- requires: 'Fact/Number N' and a headline of 2-7 words in the same sentence
- eligible when: a numbered fact is announced
- where: map, footage
- placement: numbered card
- timing: from 'Fact N'
- fallback: no headline in the sentence = no card

### place_lowerthird
- requires: a place the narrator introduces that the map can find, not the subject of the whole video
- eligible when: 'In/At/Near X' or 'X is a <kind>'
- where: map, footage
- placement: lower-left bar
- timing: from the place name
- fallback: unfindable place = nothing

### route_travel
- requires: 'from A to B' with both places found on the map
- eligible when: a journey
- where: map
- placement: route drawn on the map
- timing: from the sentence start
- fallback: a place the atlas cannot find = nothing; never over footage (the map is hidden)

### chapter_words
- requires: 'Part/Chapter/Section/Act N' announced with no title after it
- eligible when: the narrator announces a section
- where: map, footage
- placement: huge words on a dimmed frame
- timing: from the announcement
- fallback: a title after it goes to chapter_serif

### checklist_card
- requires: three to five consecutive sentences opening with First/Second/Third or Step one/two/three, each clause of 7 words and 24 characters or fewer
- eligible when: a spoken sequence
- where: map, footage
- placement: paper card on the right
- timing: from the first step to the last, +1 s
- fallback: a longer clause = no card (it is never cut)

### name_bar

- eligible when: the narrator introduces a person
- where: map, footage
- placement: lower-left bar
- timing: from the name
- fallback: a bare name or a place name = nothing
