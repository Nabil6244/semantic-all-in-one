# Book of Enoch — Visual Guide

The preset the tool follows for every Book of Enoch video: where each picture comes from, how AI scenes are made so
they do not look generated, the recurring figures, the look of the whole video, and which chapters map to which
sources. Reference style: the "Book of Enoch describes what's beneath the ocean floor" video (a new picture every
3–5 s, mostly real archival material, a few painted scenes, serif text, big numbers, quote cards).

---

## 1. Rhythm

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Shot length | 3–5 s (one narration phrase); never more than ~8 s on one picture |
| AI shots | 2–4 s each, never more than 2 AI shots in a row, about 25% of the video at most |
| Real material | the rest: manuscripts, old art, places, nature, stock |
| YouTube clips | 3–5 s, at most ~5% of the video, only footage that exists nowhere else |
| Cuts | hard cuts; a warm light-leak flash at most once every ~45 s, on a section change |
| Stills | slow push-in (Ken Burns), never a static frame |

---

## 2. Search order for every scene

1. **Manuscripts and old art** — Wikimedia Commons, museum open access, Internet Archive
2. **Places and nature** — Commons photos, NOAA, NASA, stock drone/video
3. **Stock video** — people, archives, libraries, labs, candles, hands on books
4. **AI, painting look** (section 4A)
5. **AI, photographic look** (section 4B) — only when a painting does not fit
6. **YouTube** — last resort

## 4. AI scenes that do not look AI

Only for what no camera or painter ever captured. Every prompt = **scene description + one style line + the fixed
figure description (section 5)**. No text inside images. Review every prompt in the Visual Plan before generating.

### 4A. Painting look (default for the supernatural)
Cut between real Blake / Doré / Martin works, these read as more old art.

> **Oil painting line:** *a lost 19th-century Romantic religious oil painting, dark chiaroscuro, storm light and
> firelight, muted umber, ochre and slate-blue palette, visible brushwork, fine craquelure, aged varnish, photographed
> in a museum, no text*

> **Engraving line:** *a 19th-century wood engraving in the manner of Gustave Doré, dense black-ink cross-hatching,
> dramatic light from above, aged cream paper, slight foxing, no text*

### 4B. Photographic look (only when the scene must feel "real")
> **Film line:** *35mm documentary film still, natural available light, Kodak Vision3 500T film grain, slight haze and
> dust in the air, shallow depth of field, slightly off-centre composition, muted colour, no text*

### Rules that hide AI
- **Wide and environmental:** figures small in the frame, from behind, in silhouette, or partly hidden.
- **Never:** close-up faces, hands, readable text, perfect symmetry, glossy glow, over-saturated colour.
- **Imperfections:** haze, dust, uneven light, slight blur at the edges.
- **Motion:** slow push-in; Veo image-to-video only for a few key moments, with subtle movement (smoke, fire, water,
  clouds) — never big action.
- **Placement:** always between real shots, 2–4 s.

### Automatic AI-look check (reject and regenerate)
Extra or fused fingers · garbled letters · plastic or waxy skin · melted or warped architecture · duplicated faces in a
crowd · impossible wing or limb joints · halo-like glow around everything · identical repeated patterns.

## 5. Recurring figures and places (reused word for word in every prompt)

| Figure | Fixed description | How to show |
|---|---|---|
| **Enoch** | an elderly Near-Eastern patriarch, long grey beard, plain undyed wool robe and mantle, leather sandals, a wooden staff | from behind or in profile, small in a vast landscape |
| **The Watchers** (200, led by Semjaza) | towering winged beings in dark bronze-grey robes, great dark-feathered wings, faces always in shadow or turned away | from below, in silhouette against storm light |
| **Semjaza** | the tallest Watcher, a bronze circlet, standing apart at the front | silhouette on the summit of Hermon |
| **Azazel** | a Watcher at a forge, teaching metalworking and weapons; sparks and molten metal | lit by forge light, face hidden |
| **The Nephilim (the giants)** | colossal human silhouettes in a storm, scale shown by tiny people and trees beside them | always distant, never a face |
| **Uriel (Enoch's guide)** | a tall figure of white light in a pale robe, seen from behind, leading Enoch | from behind, light source of the scene |
| **Michael, Raphael, Gabriel** | armoured figures of light, swords of light | wide, judgement scenes |
| **The house of crystal and fire** (1 Enoch 14) | a vast hall built of hailstones and ice with walls of fire, a floor of crystal | painting look |
| **The prison / the abyss** (1 Enoch 18, 21) | a chasm with pillars of fire falling into it, beyond the end of the world | painting look + real lava/vent footage |
| **Dudael** (1 Enoch 10) | a desert of rough, jagged rocks under darkness, where Azazel is bound | real Judean desert + painting look |

## 6. The look of the whole video

| Element | Setting |
|---|---|
| Colour grade (every shot, real and AI) | contrast +10%, saturation −25%, warm amber highlights, cool slate shadows, blacks slightly lifted |
| Film finish | fine 35mm grain, light vignette (~15%) |
| Quote font | a classic serif (e.g. Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond), cream on dark |
| Number / label font | a bold sans (e.g. Montserrat ExtraBold), white |
| Typed narration lines | serif, word by word with the voice, lower third |
| Transition flash | warm light leak (amber/orange), 6–10 frames |

### Graphic types for Enoch
| Graphic | Use for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Big number drop** | counts and years | 200 Watchers · 364-day year · 108 chapters · 70 generations · 1773 (James Bruce brings it to Europe) · 1821 · 1947 / 1952 (Qumran) |
| **Quote card** (paper, serif, citation) | verses from Charles 1912 | "1 Enoch 6:6", "1 Enoch 10:4" |
| **Comparison A = B** | parallels | Genesis 6 ↔ 1 Enoch 6 · 364 days ↔ Qumran calendar |
| **Label tab** | names and terms | THE WATCHERS · AZAZEL · DUDAEL · NEPHILIM |
| **Inset / polaroid frame** | a real photo inside a scene | a manuscript page, a cave, a monastery |
| **Map** (Hybrid Map / pakMap) | the route of the book | Hermon → Qumran → Ethiopia → Europe (1773) |
