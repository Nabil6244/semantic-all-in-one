# "Putin's General BREAKS SILENCE" — reverse engineering and adoption plan

Source: `Putin's General BREAKS SILENCE_ Russia Can't Stop Ukraine's Strikes!.mp4` (13:52, 1280x720, 25 fps, h264 + aac, 103 MB).

**What was checked.** 175 hard cuts found by scene detection (threshold 0.3), 208 frames sampled every 4 s (all read on labelled contact sheets), 12 full-resolution frames of every distinct graphic type, loudness of the audio. Not every one of the 20,800 frames was opened, and the audio was not transcribed (that needs your go-ahead). The shot-by-shot numbers below are counted from the sheets, not estimated from memory.

---

## 1. What this video is

A **news-analysis explainer built on a story-first script**: one real event (Gerasimov's report to Putin, 15–19 Oct) opens the video, is turned into three questions, and each question is answered with a number, a name, a place and a source. It is not a map documentary and not a weather doc. It is closest to our **News Doc** look (red kicker tag, big numbers, checklist cards, quote cards) fed with **stock footage that is darkened and blurred whenever text is on screen**, plus a **dark political map** that is revisited five times.

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Shots | ~175 cuts, average **4.7 s**, footage rarely holds past 8 s |
| Share of time that is a graphic card over dimmed/blurred footage or the map | roughly **35–40 %** (counted by eye on the sheets) |
| Share that is clean full-frame footage with a lower-third caption | roughly 60 % |
| Audio | **-14 LUFS**, loudness range only 2.4 LU, only 12 real silences: voice always on, a music bed under it, almost no dynamics |
| Footage | generic stock (US military, airports, gas pumps, Red Square). **Heavily reused**: Spasskaya Tower, St Basil's, night refinery, Pantsir truck, Gerasimov portrait each appear 3–8 times |

## 3. The visual grammar (what to copy)

1. **Footage is never replaced by a card; it is dimmed and blurred under the card.** Every text card sits over the current footage at about 20 % brightness with a strong gaussian blur and a vignette, so the screen is never a flat colour and the tone carries through the cut. This is the single biggest look difference from our News Doc.
2. **One kicker tag in the top-left on every card**: tiny red box, white spaced capitals ("IN THIS VIDEO", "GERASIMOV'S OWN FIGURES", "RUSSIA'S MOST SENIOR OFFICER"). It names the section, not the content.
3. **Palette**: red `#D61C28`-ish for danger/the enemy side, yellow `#FFD500` for the comparison baseline or the "good" figure, navy `#0E1624` rows, off-white paper `#F4EFE4` for sources. Condensed bold sans (Oswald-style) for numbers and caps, serif (Georgia/Source Serif) only on source cards, clean sans for body.
4. **Number cards are the backbone** (about 18 of them): figure first in red or yellow, label in condensed white, one small grey sub-line, a thin red rule underneath. Figures count up when they are a running tally.
5. **Lists reveal one row at a time** with a numeral, never all at once, and the list is repeated at the end as the recap.
6. **Sources are shown as paper cards** (Reuters, General Staff of Ukraine, Kyiv): red top edge, serif headline, small dark date chip, yellow highlighter on the key phrase as the narrator says it. They give the video its credibility.
7. **People are circles**: round portrait with white or yellow ring, red name plate, one-line role or quote under it. The same portrait comes back each time that person is mentioned.
8. **Lower-third caption on almost every footage shot**: black box, yellow tick, white condensed capitals, 3–7 words, typed in; it is a mini-headline for the shot (not a transcript), and often carries a date.
9. **A dark political map used five times as a recurring "base"** with country fill (red Russia, blue Ukraine, dark grey rest), pins with one-line notes, a chapter panel bottom right, camera pushes into the story location, a lower third naming the event. Between map visits the story goes to footage.
10. **Rhythm**: a visual change every ~4.7 s; the shots reused 3–8 times are what keep the cost low; nothing stays still for more than 8 s (a card holds up to 16 s only when it is a framed photo with slow drift).
11. **Sound**: voice always on, music bed under it, -14 LUFS, almost no silence. Stingers on number cards (not verified, there was no isolated audio track to test).

### Where this fits among our styles

- **News Doc / Doc Style switch** is the nearest relative: same cards, different look. Do **not** change News Doc; add a **new look pack** the Doc Style switch can select ("War Explainer"), the way Enoch and pakMap look packs already exist.
- **Hybrid Map** supplies the map and the boundary highlight; we only need a skin and a "return to the map" rule.
- **What If / StarMap** show how a style adds its own prompt, planner and look; copy that structure, not its content.
- **gfx-core** is the right home for the new templates (JS, tested by `gfx-core/test`), per the existing universal template library plan.

## 5. Plan

**Phase 0 — lock the reference (½ day, no code).** Agree this document, transcribe the voiceover once (needs your yes) to see the script structure sentence by sentence, and save 12 reference frames in `docs/styles/war_explainer_ref/` for look comparisons.

**Phase 1 — the look layer (2–3 days).**
1. "Dim + blur backdrop" for any card: gaussian blur 18–24 px, brightness 0.2–0.25, vignette; applied to the live footage under the card, with a cross-fade at card in/out. This also fixes our flat-backdrop cards everywhere.
2. Theme tokens (red/yellow/navy/paper, condensed + serif fonts we already ship) as a new look pack under Doc Style.
3. Restyle `big_number`, `checklist_card`, `rank_bars` to the pack.

**Phase 2 — new templates (3–4 days, in `gfx-core/templates/news`).**
`kicker_tag`, `source_card`, `portrait_circle`, `subscribe_pill`; the lower-third "typed headline" variant. Each with the dry-run renderability and over-footage tests that the other 79 already pass.

**Phase 3 — planner rules (3–4 days, Hybrid/doc planner).**
- agenda card ("three questions") when the script announces a list, recap card at the end;
- number card for every figure the narration states (existing statistic extractor), count-up for tallies;
- source card when the narration cites an outlet or institution (Reuters, General Staff…);
- portrait when a named person has a portrait asset, with the same portrait each time;
- shot headline lower third on 60 % of footage shots, max every other shot, never under a card;
- map revisit: return to the map at chapter boundaries (target 4–6 per 14 min), with a pin per place named;
- subscribe pill at 2:10, ~40 % and the last 40 s.

**Phase 4 — script prompt and style entry (1–2 days).** A story-first prompt (cold-open event with date and source, "three questions" agenda, one proof per question with a number, recap), added as a style next to What If/StarMap, with the Generate-scenes → Render flow they already use.

**Phase 5 — real test and compare (1–2 days).** One 10–14 min render from a script, compared frame-by-frame against the reference with the same contact sheets (cut rhythm, % card time, number of numbers, map visits). Pass criteria: average shot 4–5.5 s, 30–40 % card time, ≥ 15 number cards, ≥ 4 source cards, ≥ 4 map visits, no two cards on screen together, no still stretch over 8 s.

Total about **2–3 weeks** one person; Phases 1–2 alone already give the look.

## 6. Risks and decisions for you

1. **People** — the video depends on real portraits of Gerasimov, Zelensky, Sobyanin, Trump. We have no portrait source per video. Options: user supplies, or the tool searches Wikimedia (public domain/CC) per named person. Your rule is "no per-video asset burden", so a **cached CC portrait lookup** is the fit. Needs your call.
2. **Footage honesty** — this video shows a US Patriot battery while the narration is about Russia. We should match footage by subject and flag mismatches in the plan report rather than copy that.
3. **Numbers must come from the script** — every card's figure is read from the narration; nothing is invented (the same rule as the other styles).
4. **Scope** — new look pack only; News Doc, Weather, Hybrid and the protected manifests stay untouched (check `test_fixtures/*_protected.json` before editing, per your rule; additions in `app.py` only).
5. **Source accuracy of the reference itself** — I did not verify the claims in the video (1,398 drones, 98 %, etc.); it is a style reference, not a fact source.

Nothing in the app was changed for this review.
