# Hank Mayhew — channel DNA (reverse-engineered 2026-11-20)

Channel: youtube.com/@HankMayhew · ~41K subs · first upload ~Nov 1 2026 · 18 videos in 3 weeks.
Hit: "90 Days Without Power: The Exact Costco Cart I'd Buy Today for a Family of 4" — 1.8M views.
Studied: 5 videos downloaded (T2cDQM47DW4, JHY-d_p5VRw, H3CLfUqvq18, 50zfDwN3iPw, 0nMVgqNIKmE),
scene-cut detection on 4, frame strips inside shots, transcripts, audio floor, thumbnails.

## 1. What it really is

A **100% AI-made faceless channel wearing a face**. "Hank" is an AI character (60s, white beard,
tan cap, camo jacket, grey henley) who claims "20 years in army supply" and a "Texas ranch".

| Layer | What they use (evidence) |
|---|---|
| Host | One consistent AI character, made from a reference image (same face/clothes in every shot, every video) |
| Host talking | AI talking-head **video** — hands gesture, head moves, mouth moves (strips at 0-5 s) |
| B-roll | AI **video** clips (Veo/Kling-type): water really pours, steam rises, Hank lifts water cases. Some near-still shots = slow push-in |
| Hank in b-roll | Same character placed in scenes (garage, Costco aisle, porch, kitchen) — image-to-video from a character ref |
| Products | **Real product photos** (store listing pictures: Kirkland, Clorox, Reliance, Del Monte) on a white rounded card |
| Voice | AI voice, ~209 wpm (fast, plain talk). Transcript has `[clears throat]`, `[snorts]` — ElevenLabs v3-style audio tags left in the script |
| Music | **None.** Audio floor in pauses = -64 dB → voice only. No SFX bed heard |
| Edit | Template-driven, very regular: median shot 4.9-5.0 s, mean 5.0-5.8 s, ~10-13 cuts per minute. Hard cuts only |
| Money | Mid-roll plug for own ebook bundle (hankmayhew.com + QR code card) + description link; end subscribe button |

Volume: ~21-24 min each, one every ~1.2 days → this is a pipeline, not a person.

## 2. Shot grammar (6 shot types)

Approx. mix from 48 evenly spaced frames of the hit video:

| # | Shot | Share | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Host full** | ~3% (opening 0-15 s only) | Hank seated, waist-up, centred, in front of prepper shelves (blue water jugs, white buckets, red cans) + big US flag top-left. Warm, soft, shallow DOF |
| B | **Split screen** | ~18% | Left ~44 % = host talking (same set, tighter crop). Right ~56 % = AI b-roll of what he says. Hard vertical seam, no border |
| C | **B-roll + PiP** | ~18% | Full-frame AI b-roll + circular host cam top-right (centre ≈ 0.875w, 0.21h; Ø ≈ 0.17w; thin white ring; flag behind him). PiP shows only while he "talks to camera", drops off mid-shot |
| D | **Plain b-roll** | ~32% | Full-frame AI video: Hank doing the thing (carrying, pouring, cooking on porch), empty shelves, dark houses, lantern dinners, families walking with jugs |
| E | **Product card** | ~12% | Real product photo inside a white rounded rect (~0.3-0.5w), centred, over a heavily blurred + darkened b-roll. 1 card or 2 side by side. Slow zoom |
| F | **Data / list card** | ~17% | Darkened blurred Costco-aisle/garage bg. Title chip top-left (white box, black bold caps: "THE WATER MATH", "THE BIG THREE", "NEVER STORE IT HERE", "TRAP 2 . THE BUCKET"). Rows: white caps label left, big value right in green/red/yellow, thin rule under each row. Variants: math rows (`4 PEOPLE x 90 DAYS → 1 GAL/DAY`, `WATER YOU NEED → 360 GAL` in red), green bar chart, ✓ checklist built row by row, strike-through ("~~THE ATTIC~~"), 3-up product row with labels (RICE / BEANS / OATS) |

Rules seen:
- Opens on host full for 3 shots (5 s, 3 s, 4 s), then a dark "lights out" street, then empty Costco aisles. Hook = host + fear image + store.
- Every spoken number gets a data card; every named product gets a product card; every action gets b-roll of Hank doing it.
- Cards build line by line as the line is spoken (row 1 then row 2 appears).
- Host is never off screen for long: split or PiP every ~3-4 shots.
- Night/lantern scenes for "when it happens"; daylight garage/Costco for "what to buy".
- No captions/subtitles, no transitions, no music, no stock footage.

## 3. Script formula

- **Cold open (≈30 s):** news peg ("That's the Pentagon's plan in the headlines this week") → fear stat ("Most families won't last 90 hours") → promise ("one Costco cart, one family of four, 90 days, item by item… with the price and the reason") → **end tease** ("and at the end, the three things everybody grabs first that I'd never buy").
- **Authority line:** "20 years in army supply taught me one rule…" + folksy maxim ("You can feed a man late, but you cannot water him late").
- **Body = numbered items in buy order**, each: what → exact store item + price → math → why → one "nobody knows this" trick (water heater = 50 gal, bathtub, freezer jugs). Layers ("layer one / two / three").
- **Myth flips:** "People do the opposite… Don't be that family." "Trap 2: the bucket."
- **Daily-life walkthrough:** breakfast / lunch / dinner from the cart → proves it's livable.
- **Mid-roll:** own product plug (ebook set, QR card).
- **Payoff:** the "never buy" list promised in the open.
- **Close:** budget version ("roughly $200, 150,000 calories, 3 weeks") → one action this week → neighbour emotion line → comment question ("I read every one of them") → subscribe line with channel promise → title callback ("90 days, one cart… go get it before everybody else does").
- Voice: first person, short sentences, plain numbers, no jargon, calm-authority, slight humour. ~5,000 words for 24 min.

## 4. Titles + thumbnails

Title: `90 Days Without Power[ + twist]: The Exact <Store> Cart <promise> (<qualifier>)`
- Stores: Costco / Walmart / Dollar Tree / Tractor Supply. Twists: in an apartment, in winter, with a dog and a cat, on $100, without running water, no cell service.
- Qualifiers in brackets: "(Fits Under a Bed)", "(Half the Price)", "(All Under $3)", "(One $12 Costco Trick)".
- Openers: "NEVER…", "DON'T…".

Thumbnail: Hank waist-up holding the hero product (or pushing a loaded cart), store front behind at dusk, products piled in the foreground; 2-line text top-left, white + yellow, heavy black outline: "90 DAYS / NO POWER", "ONLY $100 / 90 DAYS", "1 CLOSET / 90 DAYS".

## 6. Built: the "Prepper Host" style (2026-11-20)

App: style picker → Explainers → **Prepper Host**. Pick a Prepper Host CSV (or a script), a voice (free Edge or your file),
the host (Dale ships with the app), host mode, sound and resolution → **Generate** (voice, shots, Flow pictures) → **Render**
(mp4 + thumbnail .jpg in the project's final folder). Command line: `python -m prepper_host.runner my.csv [--preview]`,
or `make_video.py --style prepper-host --csv my.csv`.

Write the CSV with `prepper_host/csv_prompt.txt` (and the script with `prepper_host/script_prompt.txt`).
Sample: `prepper_host/samples/water_90_days.csv` (all shot types, ~77 s).

| Piece | File |
|---|---|
| CSV format (line, shot, visual, product, title, rows, chapter) | prepper_host/csvplan.py |
| Timing, shot choice, long-line cuts, card reveal times, sound cues | prepper_host/director.py |
| Flow pictures (host refs attached), library cache, talking clips | prepper_host/flow.py |
| Host packs (Dale) | prepper_host/host.py, prepper_host/hosts/dale/ |
| Frames: host / split / pip / broll + 6 card types, chapter, name bar, subscribe, thumbnail, mix | prepper_host/render.py |
| Voice, one sentence at a time (Edge refuses some multi-sentence pieces) | prepper_host/voice.py |
| App page | ui/prepper_host_section.py |

Animations: camera moves on every picture (push in/out, pans, drift), chip slide-in, rows fade/slide up as they are said,
numbers count up, rules draw left to right, ticks pop, struck items get a red line, bars grow, product cards pop in with
an overshoot then slowly zoom, chapter bumper wipe, host name bar, round host cam pop, subscribe button click.
Sound: voice levelled, whoosh on each card, ticks/pops on rows, chapter hit, optional soft music that dips under speech.

Talking clips (fixed 2026-11-21): Flow is now agent-only (no Video switch). For a video the engine sets that project's
Agent settings → "Confirm before generating" to Never and asks plainly for one video from the attached pictures; Flow's agent
then makes a ~4 s Omni 1.2 Flash clip with Dale's face. Three clips per host are made once (out/prepper_host/hosts/<name>/talk_*.mp4)
and reused; their own sound is muted under our voice, so lips move but do not match the words exactly.

Flow voice (2026-11-21, Host mode "Flow voice"): every line on a host / split / round-cam shot is spoken by its own 8 s Flow clip
of the host saying it (pieces of up to 18 words; prepper_host/flowvoice.py). The speech is cut out of the clip, levelled and laid
into the voice track; the other lines stay on the Edge voice. Lips match on those shots. One clip per host line, kept in
out/prepper_host/library/say_*.mp4 and reused. A line Flow could not make stays on Edge. CLI: --flow-voice.
Same voice everywhere: Flow has no named voice (the video model makes one per clip), so his Flow voice is copied instead.
~20 s of speech from his spoken clips becomes out/prepper_host/hosts/<name>/flow_voice_sample.wav, and the Chatterbox clone
(Voiceover tab engine) speaks every other line from it in one model load; a line the clone misses falls back to Edge.
Flow's own voice (2026-11-21): the composer's "+" picker has a Voices tab (Algenib, Charon, Achird... the Gemini voice names).
Dale is Algenib ("Male, gravelly, low pitch"), set in his host.json with a performance note; every clip attaches it.
A clip costs 10 Flow credits (8 s, Veo 3.1 Lite); when an account has none left the agent says so in its chat and the engine
stops at once and moves on.
