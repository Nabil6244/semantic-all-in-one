1. Timing first: pictures must appear when their words are spoken (app aligner is off by up to 30 s from scene ~18 in hrt).
2. Shot director: 2-5 s shots following narration phrases; one protagonist (night-shift warehouse worker) with a fixed character sheet; 83-91 shots for hrt.
3. TRAVEL strip as the primary way pictures change: camera dwells on a picture while the narration describes it (very slow rigid drift), then travels on at the next phrase boundary (new picture centred when its first words start, travel ~1.1 s, eased); silhouette walker stands/breathes while dwelling, walks while travelling. Render the whole travel once and cut exact per-scene/per-shot slices so the app can swap each still for its slice.
4. Links for other cases (torn wipe, push-through, match cut, whip, alive dissolve) and AI image-to-video (Flow first/last frame) only on key beats (~20% of shots).
5. Audits as quality gates (no still stretch > 3 s etc.).
