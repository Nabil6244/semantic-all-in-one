# Galaxy S27 Ultra charging, explained
## Charging
The S27 Ultra keeps the same 60 watt charging as last year.
[flat: CHARGING SPEED | Galaxy S26 Ultra=60W@95 | Galaxy S27 Ultra=60W@99]
But a bigger battery should take longer to fill.
[tweet: Walle @WalleGalaxy | This stems from the new charging optimization, allowing the phone to sustain 55W longer. | sustain 55W longer]
So the real speed comes from how long it holds the peak.
