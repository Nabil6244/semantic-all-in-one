# iPhone Duo: how it looks (from the user's reference pictures, 2026-11-06)

- Book-style foldable. **Closed**: a **portrait** slab, taller than wide, about **0.71:1** (Apple's published size 84.1 x 117.8 mm; "similar in
  size to a passport"; 5.4-inch outer display), outer screen edge to edge, a **punch-hole camera top right**. Thickness 11.3 mm.
  (Corrected twice: the original text said "short and wide", then "about 0.70:1"; Apple's Tech Specs give 0.714.)
- **Open**: **landscape**, about **1.40:1** (Apple: 164.6 x 117.8 mm, 5.2 mm thick; 7.6-inch inner display), reading as two pages with a visible
  **centre crease**. (The original text said "almost square, about 7:8"; that is not supported by Apple's published dimensions.)
  Source: https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/ (read 2026-11-06). The animated models measure about 1.41:1 open and 0.70:1 closed.
- Thin flat frame (titanium / silver, or dark navy), rounded corners, flat sides; seen edge-on the two halves are visible with a hinge
  line, and a **camera module** sticks out of the back near the top. **UNRESOLVED (2026-11-06)**: two rear cameras (Apple: two lenses, microphone, flash, in a top corner);
  the old text's "one round camera bump" is not supported. **Lens arrangement, module shape and height, flash position, and the Star White back
  colour are all UNRESOLVED** against the animated models (see MODEL_VERIFICATION_DUO.md). Do not treat any of them as final.
- Colours seen: silver / natural, dark navy blue. Apple's names: **Night Sky** and **Star White** (Apple Tech Specs).

Pictures: folded_and_open.png, open_landscape.webp, side_and_outer.png.
What the test render got wrong: a tall single-slab iPhone back with a square two-lens module (normal iPhone shape), a black flat card
with an iPhone Pro module, a generic blue "fold" box. A Duo film must show the shapes above: closed (portrait, punch hole), open (landscape, about 1.40:1,
crease), and the side with the hinge.
