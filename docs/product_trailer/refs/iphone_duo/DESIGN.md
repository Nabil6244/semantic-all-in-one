# iPhone Duo: how it looks (from the user's reference pictures, 2026-11-06)

- Book-style foldable. **Closed**: short and wide (about 5:7), outer screen edge to edge, a **punch-hole camera top right**.
- **Open**: an almost square inner screen (about 7:8) with a visible **centre crease**; held in landscape it reads as two pages.
- Thin flat frame (titanium / silver, or dark navy), rounded corners, flat sides; seen edge-on the two halves are visible with a hinge
  line, and **one round camera bump** sticks out of the back near the top.
- Colours seen: silver / natural, dark navy blue.

Pictures: folded_and_open.png, open_landscape.webp, side_and_outer.png.
What the test render got wrong: a tall single-slab iPhone back with a square two-lens module (normal iPhone shape), a black flat card
with an iPhone Pro module, a generic blue "fold" box. A Duo film must show the shapes above: closed (short, punch hole), open (square,
crease), and the side with the hinge.
