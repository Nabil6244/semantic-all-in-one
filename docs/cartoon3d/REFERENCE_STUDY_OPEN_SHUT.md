## Movement

- **She is never still.** Only 1.5% of frames are near still. Measured motion repeats about every 0.3-0.4 s in the songs.
- **Editing is slow and the host carries the energy.** About 20 real cuts in 254 s, roughly one per 12 s.
- **Framing:** chest-up close shots nearly all the time. Short full-body bursts about 4 s long (20.6-24.8 s). In the bus song, full and close shots alternate about every 3 s.
- **Head:** big side tilts, lots of nods, leaning in close to the lens, the ponytail swinging hard, winks, raised brows, very wide mouth shapes, constant eye contact.
- **Hands are almost always in view,** usually at shoulder, chest or face height, often right in front of her chin and mouth.

## The picture reacts to the song

- **Background per song:** one saturated colour with a soft glow, or a flat illustrated scene.
- **Objects act out the lyric:** the spider moves on the pipe, rain capsules fall, water splashes, the bus drives in.
- **Scene mood follows the words:** at "out came the sun" the whole scene washes yellow, then returns.
- **Body part glow:** a soft orange glow appears on the cartoon baby's part as it is named.
- **Word pops:** "Beep" appears once per beep, stacked diagonally beside the bus.
- **Titles:** rounded bold text at the bottom with a thick outline, coloured for the song, up for about 4 s.
- **Picture bubbles:** a soft-edged rounded picture at top right, visible for about 5 s while she sings.
- **Real footage:** full-screen family clips of 3-6 s between songs.
- **Transitions:** mostly dissolves, plus a slide into the cloud scene.
- **Logo:** bottom left for the first 8 s or so, then it fades.

Rules taken from this:
- **A new song or verse earns a new background.** Inside a verse it stays the same, so the host is what moves.
- **Plain backgrounds are truly flat** (mint is the only one with a glow), with no patterns behind her face.
- **Colour changes with the song:** mint, pink, blue, warm house, sky blue, green hills, teal.
- **Transitions:** dissolves of 1-2 s into and out of real clips and talk; hard cuts on the downbeat of a song; one wipe in the bus song.
- **A background holds for about 2-90 s.** Songs change more often; the longest hold is the talk-free first song.
- **The wash is the one mood change:** sun yellow fades in over 1-2 s and fades out over 3 s, without any cut.

## Build order

1. **Signs:** hand shapes, hands in front of the face, a sign library, and a CSV `sign` column with automatic signs from words.
2. **Performance:** big head moves, lean-in, ponytail layer, brows, wink, wider mouth shapes.
3. **Song engine:** song rows, repeat with links, beat-locked signs, accompaniment. Needs the decision below.
4. **Song scenes:** colour sets, flat scenes, lyric-driven objects, sun wash, body part glow.
5. **Graphics and edit:** word pops, titles, bubbles, clip rows, dissolves, shot plan.

## Decision needed: where the singing comes from

The free Edge voice speaks and cannot sing. Options:
- **The user's own recording:** most natural, and the voice-over route already exists.
- **Beat-locked chant:** free and automatic, already built, but spoken in rhythm.
- **A free local singing engine:** would need a download, and needs testing for quality first.

## What stays original

Traditional rhymes (Open Shut Them, Itsy Bitsy Spider, Wheels on the Bus) are fine to use. The format, signs and techniques are adopted. Not to be copied: the name, logo, the people's likeness, their recordings, their footage or their art. "Can you say mama" and the baby body song may be their own or newly arranged songs, so Kids Learn should use its own lines there until checked.

Earlier host direction said the host "must not resemble Ms Rachel". The cartoon girl's pink tee, overalls and headband are close to this reference's signature outfit, which the user should weigh before publishing.
