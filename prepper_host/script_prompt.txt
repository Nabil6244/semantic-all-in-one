Write a ~25 minute YouTube voiceover script (about 4,800-5,200 words) for a talking-host prepper channel. The host is Dale,
a rancher in his late fifties who has kept a family fed through ice storms and long outages. First person, calm authority,
short plain sentences, real store items with real prices and quantities, a little dry humour. No headings, no stage
directions: one continuous voiceover, one paragraph per idea.

Title: <TITLE>   (pattern: "90 Days Without Power[: twist]: The Exact <Costco|Walmart|Dollar Tree|Tractor Supply> Cart <promise> (<qualifier>)")

Structure:
1. Cold open (~30 s): a news hook, the scary number ("most families won't last 90 hours"), the promise ("one cart, one
   family of four, 90 days, item by item in the order I'd load it, with the price and the reason") and an end tease
   ("and at the end, the three things everybody grabs first that I'd never buy").
2. One authority line and a folksy rule ("You can feed a man late, but you cannot water him late").
3. 6-10 items in buy order. Each: what it is, the exact store item and price, the simple math for 4 people x 90 days, why it
   earns its spot, and one trick most people miss. Use "layer one / two / three" when an item has parts.
4. One myth flip: "People do the opposite… Don't be that family."
5. A normal day off the cart: breakfast, lunch, dinner, so it is clearly not a starvation diet.
6. The promised "never buy" list, each with the reason and the cheaper swap.
7. Close: the small budget version (price, calories, weeks), one thing to do this week, the neighbour line, a comment
   question ("I read every one of them"), "subscribe to <channel>: every week, one thing that keeps your family fed, warm
   and safe when the grid goes down", and a callback to the title.

Then turn the script into a Prepper Host CSV with prepper_host/csv_prompt.txt (the lines are the script, word for word).
