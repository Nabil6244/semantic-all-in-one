## Description (paste into Customization → Basic info)

Hi friends! I'm Ms Molly! 👋🌈

Come play and learn with me, Lily, Ollie and our silly friend Bobo the Bear! Every episode is a fun adventure for toddlers and preschoolers, ages 1 to 5.

Together we:
🎨 Learn colors, shapes and numbers
🔤 Practice ABCs and first words
🐮 Meet animals and copy their sounds
🎵 Sing, clap and dance along
💛 Learn to share, take turns and be kind

Our videos ask your little one questions and pause so they can answer, repeat new words so they stick, and get them moving with songs and dances. Parents, sing along too!

New episodes every week. Subscribe so you never miss a playtime! 🐻🎈

All characters, songs and stories are original.
