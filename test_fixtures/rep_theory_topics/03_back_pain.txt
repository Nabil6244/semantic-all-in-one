Title: Fix Lower Back Pain From Sitting All Day
If your lower back aches after a long day at your desk, you are not broken. Your back is just stuck in one position for too long. Here are four moves that take five minutes.
Sitting for hours makes your hip flexors tight and switches off your glutes. Then your lower back does work it was never meant to do.
Move one is the hip flexor stretch. Kneel on one knee, squeeze the glute of the back leg, and push your hips forward gently. Hold for thirty seconds on each side.
Move two is the glute bridge. Lie on your back, feet flat, and lift your hips until your body forms a straight line from your knees to your shoulders. Do two sets of twelve.
Move three is the bird dog. On hands and knees, reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back. Keep your back flat, like a table. Do ten on each side.
Move four is the cat and cow. Round your spine slowly, then arch it. This gets the stiff joints moving again.
The biggest mistake is stretching your lower back hard when it hurts. Often the problem is your hips, not your back.
Also, get up every thirty minutes. Even one minute of walking resets your posture.
If you feel numbness down your leg, or the pain is sharp and getting worse, see a doctor. That is not something to stretch away.
Do these every day for two weeks and tell me in the comments if your back feels different.
