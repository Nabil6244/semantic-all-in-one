Title: Ten Minutes a Day to Unlock Tight Hips
Tight hips make your squat shallow, your back sore and your walk stiff. The good news is that hips respond quickly to a little daily work.
Your hips can move in six directions. Most of us only use one of them, walking forward. The rest slowly get stiff.
Start with the ninety-ninety. Sit with both knees bent at ninety degrees, one in front and one to the side. Lean over the front leg and breathe for one minute. Then switch.
Next, the deep squat hold. Hold onto a door frame, sit down as low as you can, and let your hips relax there. Stay for one minute.
Then the frog stretch. On hands and knees, slide your knees wide apart and rock your hips back slowly.
Finish with leg swings. Swing each leg forward and back, then side to side, fifteen times.
The mistake is forcing the stretch. If you push into pain, your muscles tighten up to protect you. Go to a mild pull and breathe.
Another mistake is stretching only once a week. Little and often beats long and rare.
In one study, people who stretched daily for six weeks gained clear range of motion in their hips.
Try it for a month and comment how deep your squat gets.
