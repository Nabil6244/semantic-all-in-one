Title: Get Your First Pull-Up
Can't do a single pull-up? Most people can't at the start. Here is a plan that takes you from zero to your first rep.
A pull-up mainly uses your lats, the big muscles on the sides of your back, with help from your biceps and your grip.
Stage one is the dead hang. Hang from the bar for thirty seconds. Three times. If your grip gives up first, you have found your first job.
Stage two is the scapular pull. While hanging, pull your shoulder blades down without bending your arms. Your body rises a couple of centimeters. Do three sets of eight.
Stage three is the negative. Jump to the top, chin over the bar, and lower yourself as slowly as you can. Aim for five seconds down. Five reps.
Stage four is the band pull-up. Loop a band over the bar and put your knee in it. As you get stronger, use a thinner band.
Train this three times a week. Most people get their first pull-up in six to ten weeks.
The most common mistake is kicking and swinging to get up. It feels like progress, but your back isn't doing the work.
Another mistake is training to failure every time. Leave one rep in the tank.
