Title: Fix Forward Head Posture
Look at your side profile in a photo. If your head sits in front of your shoulders, you have forward head posture. And your neck is paying for it.
Every inch your head moves forward adds weight that your neck has to hold. At a steep angle, it can feel like carrying a child on your neck all day.
This comes from screens. Phones pull your head down, and laptops pull it forward.
Here is the fix, in three parts.
Part one, chin tucks. Pull your chin straight back like you are making a double chin. Hold for five seconds. Do ten.
Part two, wall angels. Stand with your back flat against a wall and slide your arms up and down, keeping your wrists and elbows touching the wall. Do two sets of ten.
Part three, strengthen your upper back with rows. Use a band or a backpack, and squeeze your shoulder blades together. Three sets of fifteen.
The common mistake is pulling your shoulders back hard and arching your lower back. That just moves the problem.
Instead, raise your screen to eye level. Your head follows your eyes.
Most people notice a difference in about four weeks.
Take a side photo today, and another one in a month.
