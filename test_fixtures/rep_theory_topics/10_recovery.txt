Title: Are You Overtraining or Just Tired?
More training is not always better. Muscles break down when you train and rebuild when you rest. Skip the rest and you just keep breaking down.
Here are the warning signs. Your strength drops for more than a week. Your resting heart rate is five or more beats higher than usual in the morning. You sleep badly and feel irritable. Your joints ache and small injuries won't heal.
True overtraining is rare. What most people have is under-recovery: too little sleep, too little food and too much stress.
So what helps? First, a deload. Every four to six weeks, cut your sets in half for one week. Most people come back stronger.
Second, eat enough. Training hard on a big calorie deficit is the fastest way to stall.
Third, active recovery. A light walk or easy bike ride increases blood flow without adding fatigue.
What about ice baths? They can reduce soreness, but right after strength training they may blunt muscle growth. Save them for when you need to recover fast between games.
The mistake is ignoring the signs and pushing harder. That is how a tired week becomes an injured month.
Check your morning heart rate for a week and comment what you see.
