Title: The Truth About Losing Belly Fat
You can't choose where you lose fat. Doing a thousand sit-ups will not burn the fat on your stomach. A study had people train only one leg for twelve weeks. They lost fat, but evenly from their whole body.
So how do you lose belly fat? You lose fat everywhere, and your belly comes along.
Step one is a small calorie deficit. Eat about three hundred to five hundred calories less than you burn each day. That's about half a kilogram a week.
Step two is lifting weights. When you diet without strength training, up to a quarter of the weight you lose can be muscle. Lifting tells your body to keep it.
Step three is walking. Eight to ten thousand steps a day burns more than most people's workouts.
Step four is protein at every meal, because it keeps you full.
The biggest mistake is cutting calories too hard. A crash diet makes you hungry, tired and likely to quit within weeks.
Another mistake is trusting the scale every day. Your weight can jump a kilogram overnight from water alone. Look at the weekly average instead.
Belly fat is often the last to go, especially for men. Be patient.
Give it twelve weeks and measure your waist, not just your weight.
