Title: Run Your First 5K Without Hating It
Most beginners quit running in the first month. Not because they're unfit, but because they run too fast. Slow down and running gets easy.
Here is the rule. You should be able to talk in full sentences while you run. If you can't, you are going too fast.
Week one, run for one minute, then walk for two minutes. Repeat that eight times. That's it.
Week two, run two minutes and walk one minute. Week three, run five minutes and walk one. By week six most people can run for twenty five minutes without stopping.
Run three days a week with a rest day in between. Your heart adapts quickly, but your tendons and your shins need more time.
Your form matters less than you think. Take short, quick steps and land with your foot under your body, not far in front of it.
The most common mistake is adding distance too fast. A good rule is not more than ten percent more each week.
Shin pain is a warning sign. If it hurts to press on the bone, take a few days off.
What about shoes? Pick the pair that feels comfortable in the store. Comfort predicted fewer injuries than any special feature.
And breathe through your mouth and nose together. You need the air.
Run your first 5K in eight weeks and comment your finish time.
