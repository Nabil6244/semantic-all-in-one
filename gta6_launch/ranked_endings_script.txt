One ending in GTA 6 finishes with Jason and Lucia in a swamp. Another only unlocks because of something you did hours before the final mission. And the internet cannot agree whether this game has four endings, five, or six. Spoilers ahead, so pause here if you have not finished the story. I am ranking every ending from worst to best, showing you what unlocks each one, and flagging every spot where the sources disagree. Let us get into it. First, the count, because it changes how we rank. Reports published between November 20 and November 22 give four endings, five endings, or six, and one outlet manages to say both four and six in the same article. GamerBlurb and Detonated both list five, The Big Lead lists four, and ComicBook lists six. Rockstar has not published a number. Beebom reports that Rockstar said the campaign holds a lot more than two conclusions, but I could not trace that to a first party source, so treat it as reported, not confirmed. Here is what the sources do agree on. The finale is a mission called The End of the Road. Jason and Lucia hit a police roadblock on a bridge in the Keys, and you pick between Swim for It and No Surrender. Everything else depends on your criminal profile, how close the two of them are, how much money sits in the BuckMe family savings, and a couple of earlier choices. A quick explainer on criminal profiles, since they decide so much. Each of the two leads has one. Careful, low casualty play keeps it at Professional, and going in guns blazing pushes it toward Psycho, or even Irredeemable, according to The Big Lead. Those top two tiers are what close the good doors. I am using the six ending layout that three outlets share, ComicBook, Beebom, and Of Zen and Computing, and the names shift a little from site to site. Smaller variants exist, and I will point them out as we go. Number six, the worst, and it is a secret ending. Some outlets call it The Swamp, others call it Alligator Lunch. It starts well before the bridge. When you confront Mendez, the game offers you a shoot option first. If you hold off, wait for the second prompt, and take his deal, you are on this path. According to those reports, Mendez drives Jason and Lucia into the swamps of Leonida and kills them, he gets promoted, and their son is orphaned. One outlet says you only see their skulls after the credits. It ranks last because nothing you did in the story pays off, and the only winner is Mendez, who gets promoted. Number five is No Surrender, sometimes called Bonnie and Clyde. At the bridge you pick No Surrender, and the shootout cannot be won. Both of them die in the police ambush. ComicBook says that saving around two and a half million in the family account gives their son a better chance at support, but he still grows up without them. This is the ending the Bonnie and Clyde comparisons seemed to promise. It is tragic, but it is also the most predictable one, which is why it sits above the swamp and below everything else. Number four is where the sources contradict each other the hardest. It is called the Botched Surrender. You pick Swim for It, but a Psycho or Irredeemable criminal profile means the police refuse the surrender and open fire anyway. ComicBook says both are shot. The Big Lead says Lucia trades the hard drive for a pardon and walks free while Jason is killed. Of Zen and Computing says one or both die. So treat the exact outcome with caution. What they all agree on is the cause, and it is your own chaos catching up with you. That is a brutal piece of design, and a smart one. Now we are in the top three, and this is where the endings start to hurt in a different way. Number three is Staying Down. This one splits off earlier, at a tower standoff, after you take Mendez's deal and fight your way out of the ambush. Jason is shot after pushing Lucia down. Lucia takes the government deal and reunites with her son, but if the savings are not there, she is shown robbing a store afterward. Sources call it bittersweet, or neutral. It lands in the middle because Jason's death feels earned by the story and Lucia survives, but her future looks thin. Number two is Prison Sacrifice. You choose Swim for It with a profile below Psycho. Jason stays behind so Lucia can escape, and he is arrested. Lucia gets back to Tomas, raises him, and visits Jason in prison, depending on how close they are. One outlet says he may be released one day. Everyone survives, and the price is years apart. It ranks second because it is the first ending where somebody chooses the other person on purpose. Number one is the ending that asks the most of you, so stay for the requirements. It is called Lucia Pays Jason's Bail, or Happily Ever After, and every ranked list I read calls it the best. You need the top relationship tier, a clean criminal profile, which The Big Lead says means Professional or Hardened, a large balance in BuckMe, and Swim for It at the bridge. The savings number is where the reports disagree, with targets ranging from two million to three million, and the bail itself is reported at two and a half million, so check your own balance before you go in. According to the reports, Lucia gets away, Jason serves time, she pays his bail, and the family starts over, with Liberty City reported as their new home. It wins because it is the only ending where the whole run of choices, the relationship, the money, and the restraint, comes back to pay you. One more detail before the rumors. Detonated, which says its own list is incomplete, reports that every ending shares the same post credit scenes. Mattia and Kat have a son, and Cal gets an email from an unknown contact, with the suggestion that the stolen hard drive data was sent to him. That is one outlet's reading, so call it reported. Now let us separate the rumors from the facts, because the ending has been a leak target for months. On October 14, Dexerto reported that Cyberleek posted a short video of Lucia in mission footage and said he has the ending. That is his claim. Nobody has confirmed the clips come from the final missions, or that he holds the ending, and Rockstar has not commented on those new posts. There is also an older rumor from June 2025, that Rockstar might rewrite the ending after leaks, based on an anonymous source speaking to the leaker Tez2. It was never confirmed. We now have a released game with multiple endings, which proves nothing either way about a rewrite, so I am filing that one under unconfirmed. One last thing worth knowing. Kotaku reported on November 18 that you can keep playing after the credits. Before the final mission, the game asks if you want to go ahead. After you see an ending, you are returned to that choice. The finale marker stays on the map, so you can go back for a different outcome. So if you hate the one you got, no ending on this list is locked in, as long as you kept a save before the finale. GamerBlurb says the game gives you fifteen manual save slots, which helps. Here is the full ranking one more time. The swamp at six, No Surrender at five, the Botched Surrender at four, Staying Down at three, Prison Sacrifice at two, and Lucia paying Jason's bail at the top. Which one did you get first?
