GTA Six Chapter Two drops you at the Starlight Motel with under a hundred and fifty dollars. Linda is already waiting at the front desk with a nine-hundred-dollar bill. Meanwhile, Rockstar hid ten insane early-game secrets right under your nose and never put them in a tutorial. We are talking Paul Walker's free green Eclipse, a talking parrot guarding thirteen hundred bucks, and a hidden serial killer mission with zero map markers. Wait until you hear trick number two, because one wrong click at Moses's garage will cost you nearly seven thousand dollars.

Trick number one: grab Paul Walker's iconic green Mitsubishi Eclipse for free before doing anything else. In-game, it is called the Maibatsu ninety-seven Penumbra, complete with the bright green paint, blue wave graphics, and massive rear spoiler. Head over to Vespucci Island in the Rialto Islands just east of Vice City. Drive along the eastern beach until you hit the bridge, stop at the first crossroad, turn left, and look at the first house on your right. Walk up to the small side door next to the garage and force it open, or pop it with a Snap Gun. Now stop—do not jump straight into the car when the garage opens. Find the alarm switch on the far wall and cut it first, or the cops get pinged the second you roll out. Once the alarm is dead, smash the window, hotwire the Eclipse, and peel out with one of the fastest starter cars in Leonida.

That leads straight into trick number two, the single biggest rookie mistake in Chapter Two. When you meet Moses the mechanic, he offers to register your very first vehicle completely free. Most players pull up in whatever beat-up street car they stole ten minutes ago and burn that free slot forever. If you waste it and bring Paul Walker's Eclipse to Moses later, he charges you six thousand seven hundred and fifty dollars out of your BuckMe account. So do it in this exact order: steal the Eclipse first, finish Moses's intro mission, and hand him the Eclipse as your free registration. Just remember two quick catches: Moses refuses any car with a tracker, and you will need to buy a four-thousand-dollar garage space on your phone property app to store it safely.

Now if you do not feel like driving all the way out to Vespucci Island in a slow junker, use trick number three. Open the RydeMe app on your in-game phone and book a ride straight across town. Once the driver pulls up, pay the extra one hundred dollars to skip the journey and teleport straight to the island instantly. Leave your old starter car behind—you are driving back in the green Eclipse anyway. Just make sure your BuckMe account actually has money in it, because RydeMe will not take cash out of your pocket.

Need instant pocket money right at the start of the game? Trick number four is genuinely hilarious. Talk to your neighbor Shirley early on, and she sends you down to Three Sheets Liquor to pick up some rum. While you are inside buying the bottle, listen closely—the shop owner yells at noisy animals locked in a back room. Deliver the rum to Shirley first, then head right back to Three Sheets Liquor. Whatever you do, do not try walking behind the counter, because the owner will immediately flip out. Instead, hop the fence around the side of the building and clear out the criminals hanging around out back. You will find a bright red talking parrot trapped in a cage—and if you listen to what it squawks, it drops clues about cash and a generator. Walk right over to the generator, check the grate hidden in the wall next to it, and pull out over thirteen hundred dollars in free cash.

Okay, thirteen hundred bucks pays Linda off—but what if you want fast repeatable cash for guns and a garage? Trick number five: drive your new Eclipse straight over to Gellhorn Raceway Motorsports Park. Pick the short loop race—it is only three quick laps and takes just over two minutes from start to finish. You can rent a track car if you want, but bringing your own Eclipse skips the rental fee completely. Wait until the final corner, nudge the inside line to take first place, and you pocket two thousand five hundred dollars per win. Run it three or four times in ten minutes and your early-game money problems are completely solved.

Before you celebrate that racetrack money, remember trick number six or you will learn it the hard way. In GTA Six, if you get wasted or busted by the cops, the cash sitting in your pocket is completely gone. On top of that, major early-game services like Moses's garage and the RydeMe app only accept digital BuckMe transfers, not paper cash. So after every race or stash pickup, stop at the nearest ATM or open your banking app and deposit everything immediately. Also keep one eye on the Eclipse's bumper—it is insanely fast for escaping cops, but it takes crash damage quickly, so do not treat it like a tank.

Now let's grab easy heist money in Port Gellhorn with trick number seven: all three Chapter Two safe codes. Yes, you can blow locked safes open with Sticky Bombs, except explosions instantly alert every enemy nearby and bring the cops down on you. Instead, here are the exact silent codes you can punch in right now: Brooks Boatworks is six-eight-two-three, La Cascara Motel is six-nine-six-nine, and Kingpin Bowling is zero-three-zero-zero. Even better, if you want the game to auto-display all three codes on your HUD forever, sneak through the hole in the east fence at Brooks Boatworks. Head up the stairs of the gray building, walk inside, and hold L2 on the wall map—and GTA Six automatically logs all three safe codes for you.

Next up, trick number eight: three hidden driving shortcuts that even players a hundred and fifty hours deep are just discovering. Tired of opening a clunky radial menu just to shut off the car radio? Double-tap Left on the D-pad and Jason or Lucia toggles the radio instantly. Want to kill your headlights when hiding from cops at night? Double-tap Circle on PlayStation or B on Xbox to cycle between off, low beams, and high beams. And when Leonida's crazy NPC drivers cut you off in traffic, click the Right Stick in. Rockstar literally built in a secret road rage button so Jason and Lucia can yell straight out the window at bad drivers.

Wait—Rockstar hid one more double-tap shortcut that actually wins gunfights, and that is trick number nine. While driving any car, double-tap Triangle on PlayStation or Y on Xbox to hop out while leaving the engine and radio running. Do that at full speed toward a gang hideout, and your character dives safely out the side while your car slams straight into the enemy barricade like a guided missile. And it works on water too: double-tap Triangle or Y while steering a boat, and you can stand up and walk around the deck while the boat keeps cruising across Vice City on autopilot.

Finally, trick number ten—easily the creepiest hidden mission in Chapter Two, and almost everyone walks right past it. Inside the motel, you will spot missing-tourist posters that add a Port Gellhorn tipline to your phone—except nobody picks up and zero markers appear on your map. To trigger it, drive to the warehouse bunker just east of Moses's shop in Port Gellhorn. The front shutter slams shut halfway, so crawl through the hole in the right-side wall to find a serial killer's shrine with three vague victim photos. Here are all three exact locations so you do not search for hours: victim one is inside the trunk of a bright pink car with a PRTYGRL plate at the junkyard east of the Port Gellhorn Pay 'n' Spray. Victim two is hidden beneath the floorboards of the first enterable trailer by the giant water tower north of town. Victim three is inside a submerged car in the alligator-packed lake west of Port Gellhorn near Wild Hog Point. Call the tipline at each body, then return to the bunker to meet Alvan West—he tells you to check the fifth locker, where you find a photo of Jason and Lucia. After he hits you with chloroform, you switch to your partner, track him to an eerie mine, dig your partner out from under a pile of rocks, and take Alvan down for good.
