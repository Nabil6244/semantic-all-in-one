Every GTA game has UFO Easter eggs floating miles up in the sky where you can never touch them. In Grand Theft Auto Six, Rockstar flipped the script, and hid a full three-part playable alien abduction mission. If you drive down the wrong rural road after two in the morning, a glowing UFO drops out of the fog and abducts Jason or Lucia. That abduction triggers a playable white-void dream with a secret four-digit keypad code, leading straight into an underground Grassrivers alien lab. Even crazier, players just solved Rockstar's five-year-old San Andreas UFO house mystery inside Belville, and the garage hides a disturbing secret. Here is the exact step-by-step guide to trigger the abduction, unlock the lab, claim four permanent stat upgrades, and find the UFO house.

Step one starts as soon as you finish Chapter One and unlock free roam in Kelly County. Wait until after two in the morning in-game, make sure you are completely alone, and ride east out of Port Gellhorn near Watkins Auto Parts. Watch the shoulder of the road for a stranded twenty-twenty Declasse Impaler sitting with its door hanging open and headlights flashing into the trees. Stop your ride and use the shout button to call out, nobody answers. You will spot a trail of scattered clothes leading straight off the pavement into the dark, foggy woods. Follow those clothes deeper between the trees until the entire forest suddenly explodes in blinding blue-white light. A massive UFO is hovering right above the canopy, aborting an abduction and dropping a confused, naked woman onto the dirt. Talk to her and offer to fetch her car back on the road, because the second you walk back to her vehicle, the UFO snatches you instead. Right after the abduction cuts back to the road, the secret trophy 'The Truth Is Out There' pops on your screen.

Do not think the mission is over after that trophy pops, because part two triggers the next time your abducted character sleeps at the Starlet Motel. Instead of waking up normally, you drop into a playable dream sequence inside a stark white void lined with glowing capsules. Walk up to the round window and zoom in, you are looking straight out at a fenced compound beside a giant radio tower. Now look closely at the wall above the dream keypad, because Rockstar hid your exact entry code in glowing light bars. Count each cluster of bars from left to right, in most playthroughs they group as three, two, one, four for code three-two-one-four, though some runs generate two-one-three-four. Punch your light-bar digits into the dream keypad, and GTA Six automatically saves the code in your phone's Notes app. When Lucia wakes up, she tells Jason about the warning signs and the towering radio mast, unlocking the hidden exploration task: Find the Mysterious Place.

For part three, bring both Jason and Lucia down into the Grassrivers along Boundary Road, between Port Gellhorn and South Point Airfield near Key Lento. Look for a small guard booth, chain-link fencing covered in warning signs, and the massive radio tower from Lucia's dream. Head through the gate or climb the left side of the fence, then climb the ladder on the far side of the main building onto the roof. Drop down through the open roof hatch and head downstairs until you hit a heavy door marked Restricted Area with a glowing keypad. Type in your dream code, three-two-one-four or two-one-three-four straight from your phone notes, and step into the elevator. The lift drops you deep underground into a full-blown Area Fifty-One style alien research facility.

Once you reach the basement control room, play the audio tapes sitting on the console. They reveal the lab has been holding a live creature labeled Subject N-H-I Zero-One, and finally explain why GTA aliens keep abducting cows: they feed it raw cow's milk. Press the control panel buttons until every light turns green, then pull the lever to unlock the three sealed lab doors. Move fast here, because stepping through the middle door hits you with lingering radiation that steadily drains your health bar. Duck through the left corridor, grab the bottle of raw cow's milk off the desk, and sprint back through the control room into the right-hand chamber. Insert the milk bottle into the containment capsule, it hisses open, revealing a live alien lying right on a gurney. The instant you pick the alien up, alarms scream, a security breach triggers, and toxic gas floods the entire floor. Do not stop to loot, sprint straight back to the elevator and ride it to the surface before the gas drops you.

As soon as you burst back out onto the grass, secret government agents roll up to arrest you. Before a firefight even starts, the UFO roars back overhead above the radio tower, beams up your friendly alien, and scares the agents off. Then the ship bathes Jason and Lucia in bright beam light, and hands you one of the best permanent rewards in GTA Six. Four instant stat upgrades pop on screen: Focus increased, Maximum Focus increased, Maximum Stamina increased, and Maximum Health increased. That makes this secret UFO quest mandatory early in Chapter Two, not just for the trophy, but for permanent health and Focus bars.

Wait, before you click off, there is one more UFO secret players just cracked five years after Rockstar first teased it. Back in twenty-twenty-one, Rockstar slipped a mysterious framed photo into GTA San Andreas Definitive Edition showing a UFO hovering over a cream bungalow with two palm trees. For five years nobody knew where that house was, until launch weekend, when players found the exact bungalow standing in Belville. Walk into its open garage during the day, and the entire wall is covered in a massive conspiracy corkboard screaming in giant red letters: 'It's a simulation.' Surrounding it are frantic notes from a sentient NPC asking 'Why can't I leave Leonida?', 'Number six, what does it mean?', 'Who is L, who is J?', and 'Identical People: one hundred and forty.' Even creepier, boat brochures are scattered across the floor, hinting that the owner tried sailing past the edge of the map to escape the game.
