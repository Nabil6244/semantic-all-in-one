Three things are about to ruin your first night in Leonida, and one of them is a cop who remembers the color of your shirt. Another is a TV setting that delays every button you press, and it may be switched on right now. The third is a download bar that is still crawling while everyone else is already playing. All three have fixes, ten in total, and I will give them to you in the order you need them. Stay to the end, because the preload files already show what is sitting inside those gigabytes, and one clue explains a setting I am going to ask you to leave alone. Start with the download bar. Back in 2013, GTA 5 on the Xbox 360 came on two discs, one to install from and one to play from. GTA 6 has no disc at all. Rockstar says it needs up to 212 gigabytes depending on your console, and even the boxed edition is just a download code. Early unboxings show that code, a double-sided poster, and no paper map. So how long will 212 gigabytes really take? On a 100 megabit connection, close to five hours. On 600 megabits, about 45 minutes. Those are perfect-world numbers, and launch week is never a perfect world, so if your internet is slow, the preload is the only thing that matters. Digital pre-orders could start preloading on November 10th. Open GTA 6 in your library and confirm the progress bar is actually moving. If it is not, start it yourself. And if you can, run a cable to the console instead of trusting Wi-Fi. That is fix one. Fix two is space, and this is where people get caught. Data miners reading the PlayStation package reported about 223 gigabytes, eleven more than Rockstar's figure, and nobody has explained that gap yet. The end of this video shows what some of those gigabytes really are. Add a likely day-one patch on top and I would want 230 gigabytes free. If you are short, do not delete a single game yet. Check your capture gallery first, because trophy clips save themselves automatically, and after a few years they can eat a shocking amount of your drive. Sort by size, clear the big ones or move them to a USB stick, then switch trophy videos off in the capture settings. If you still need room, buy an internal SSD, because a USB drive can store PS5 games but it cannot play them. Fix three is to let the console work while you sleep. In the power saving settings, turn on staying connected to the internet in rest mode and keep automatic downloads on. Do your console and controller updates today as well, so nothing new is waiting for you on launch night. So the download is handled. But here is the question nobody asks until it is too late. What happens when the game is installed and it still feels wrong? That is problem two, the screen. On November 10th, Rockstar confirmed that GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on every console, the PS5 Pro included. The Pro gets a sharper image from PSSR, Sony's AI upscaler, plus ray traced shadows you will not see anywhere else. Every PS5 gets haptic feedback across the whole game, Tempest 3D audio, and faster loading. Thirty frames is fine, but it means any delay your TV adds is easier to feel, and that brings us to fix four. Turn on game mode and turn off motion smoothing. Motion smoothing invents extra frames between the real ones, and to do that it holds your picture back, which is the exact opposite of what you want. Fix five is checking that you are really getting 4K. On PS5, open screen and video. One page shows the signal your console is sending, and the other shows what your TV can accept. If a 4K TV never offers 2160p, something between the two is holding the signal back, most often an old HDMI cable or a soundbar passing it through. Fix six is the HDR calibration. It takes about a minute, and you just raise each pattern until the symbol barely fades out. Then leave VRR and 120 hertz on automatic. Why would I tell you to keep a 120 hertz setting on for a 30 frame game? Hold that thought, because the answer is waiting in the files. Fix seven is sound. Turn on 3D audio in the sound settings and it works with whatever wired headphones you plug into the controller. If you use a soundbar or a surround system, make sure the output is not set to stereo. Keep haptics and adaptive triggers on, and if they get tiring, there is a slider in accessibility. Then charge the controller and clear the dust from the console's vents before your first long session. And if you are still on an Xbox One, you are not locked out. GTA 6 streams through Xbox Cloud Gaming, reportedly at Series S settings, once you own the game. The catch is time. This month, Game Pass Ultimate started capping cloud play at 15 hours a month, and Microsoft has not said how GTA 6 counts against that. VGC reported that existing members in some countries, including Australia, Germany and Brazil, got emails saying the cap will not apply to them as long as they keep their subscription running. The US, the UK and Canada are not on that list. If you live in one of those three, fifteen hours is a single weekend of GTA, so treat the cloud as a backup plan and not the plan. Now the clock, because it decides who sees what first. Rockstar says GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19th. That put New Zealand first, a full thirteen hours ahead of the UK. Forbes found that some US storefronts run on Eastern time, so on the West Coast it may have opened at 9 p.m. on the 18th. Great if you are in California, terrible if you are dodging spoilers. The first clips from New Zealand were online while most of us were at work, so mute GTA 6, Jason and Lucia on X, TikTok and Instagram for a couple of days. And please skip the so-called leaked build. A 113 gigabyte GTA 6 file has been going around since the summer, and security testers found it is almost entirely empty padding with a small piece of malware inside. There is no real build out there, and cheap accounts are the same kind of trap. Which brings us to the problem that can cost you the most, and the game will not warn you about it: the cop who remembers your shirt. According to the previews, you do not start rich. You start in the Leonida Keys with space for about three cars, and the police work from a description. They track your clothes, your face, your car, and whether you have a partner with you. A hat or sunglasses is not enough. A full change of clothes is, and previewers say you can keep one in your trunk. So fix eight is a spare outfit, and it should be the first thing you carry. Haircuts are less clear. Some reviewers say a new cut clears your face, others say it does not, so I would lose the cops first and test the haircut later. Fix nine is where you sell. Previews describe a phone app that scans a car and tells you whether it has an alarm or a tracker, and what it is worth. Older cars open with a slim jim, newer ones need a key cloner, and that only unlocks later in the story. Paint spray is back, but specialty fences pay for the good stuff. If the car has a tracker, lose it before you drive anywhere you care about. Fix ten is the money itself. Previews say the cash you are carrying is gone if you die or get arrested, and only what is in the bank is safe. So deposit early. Stolen cars you never register eventually vanish too. Put those two together and the best early purchase is somewhere to keep things. That is why I would grab the pre-order pack before November 20th if you have not bought the game yet. Alongside a 55 Vapid Stanier, outfits and a weapon pattern, it reportedly includes the Shore Court garage right by Ocean Beach, and of everything in the pack, that garage is the part you will use on day one. Finally, the preload files, and I promised you the clue. They are encrypted, so data miners can only see names and sizes, and Rockstar has not confirmed any of this. Tez2 found the story sitting in its own chunk, called story zero, at about 18 gigabytes, and says the online side hangs off the same structure. That is why some people think story and online could install separately. Audio alone is over 22 gigabytes. And the files reportedly mention 120 hertz output. On a 30 frame game, the usual reason for that is a 40 frames per second mode, which needs a 120 hertz TV. Rockstar's Rob Nelson has only promised 30 at launch, so treat it as a maybe, but it is exactly why I told you to leave that setting on. That is every fix I have got. Now it is your turn.
