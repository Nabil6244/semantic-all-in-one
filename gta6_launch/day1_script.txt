Number nine. Committing a crime in front of the wrong person. The wanted system has been rebuilt around information. Nelson told Famitsu that this builds on the witness idea from Red Dead Redemption 2, which means a crime nobody sees can go unreported. Each detail the police lock in, like your appearance, your outfit, your weapon or your car, shows up as a small icon below the wanted level. So before you pull a gun in a shop, look for the camera in the corner and the person at the counter, because a quiet job and a six star manhunt can be the same job with a different witness.
