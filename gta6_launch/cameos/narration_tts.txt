Rockstar swore Grand Theft Auto Six was a fresh start, until players started spotting familiar faces across Leonida. Within seventy-two hours of launch, fans uncovered an actual playable-era GTA Four protagonist returning in a Chapter Five cutscene, Michael De Santa's son trying to sneak past your nightclub velvet rope, and a bizarre radio broadcast that sounds exactly like Trevor Philips. Even crazier, Rockstar hid Arthur Morgan's cowboy hat at a backyard yard sale, a tiny cowboy riding a Chihuahua, and a full four-part Dexter Morgan serial killer Easter egg in Vice Beach. Here is every single returning GTA, Red Dead, and Dexter cameo confirmed in GTA Six, and exactly where to find them.

Let's start with the jaw-dropper nobody saw coming: Niko Bellic from GTA Four is officially in Grand Theft Auto Six. You do not play as him, and Rockstar even hides his name in the credits by listing him simply as The Hitman. Wait until Chapter Five during the mission Taking Liberties, and Niko steps straight out of the golden swamp fog wearing sunglasses and a floral Hawaiian shirt. Even better, original voice actor Michael Hollick actually returned to voice him. He dismisses the guards, checks on Jason and Lucia, pats his tactical dog Rocco, and delivers the coldest parting line in the game: Well, I guess it's time to go home, I was beginning to like it down here. After eighteen years of silence from Liberty City, hearing Niko's voice again hits like a truck.

Next up is the only physical GTA Five character confirmed in the main story so far: Michael's son, Jimmy De Santa. During the Chapter Four mission Headliners, you are working the velvet rope as security outside a packed Vice City nightclub. Look closely at the crew in matching hoodies trying to flex a company card, that is an older Jimmy De Santa standing right in front of you. He even brags about working on Righteous Slaughter with Misfire Games, a direct nod to the shooter he played non-stop in Michael's living room back in Los Santos. The funniest part is that Rockstar hands the choice straight to you: you can wave Jimmy inside the VIP section or turn him away at the door. Either way, it is a one-scene cameo, so do not blink or you will miss him.

Now here is the big question everyone is asking: what about Michael, Franklin, and Trevor? Officially, none of the three protagonists, or their actors Ned Luke, Shawn Fonteno, and Steven Ogg, appear in the GTA Six cast credits. Yet Rockstar still snuck in two wild audio callbacks that broke the internet on launch day. First, listen to a hidden true-crime audio interview where an investigator grills Wade about a locker photograph of three dangerous men. Wade nervously refuses to say Trevor's name, calling him his best friend with a teddy bear in his truck, while naming Michael De Santa as a family man and Franklin Clinton as a street rascal, swearing they never killed Floyd and Debra. Second, tune into Weazel News on the radio and you will hear Vice City Mayor Donnie Profanaldi ranting about twenty tons of seized cocaine. Players swore it was Steven Ogg returning as Trevor in disguise, even though Ogg is uncredited, making it GTA Six's biggest unsolved voice mystery.

If you miss Red Dead Redemption Two, wait until you unlock Chapter Two and drive down to southern Port Gellhorn in Kelly County. Cruise along Orchard Avenue near Wild Hog Point until you spot a blue house with colorful balloons and a tall American flag hosting a backyard yard sale. Walk past the folding tables straight to the white couch on the driveway, sitting right on top of a black guitar case is a brown leather hat labeled Genuine Cowboy Hat. The worn brim and braided leather cord are a one-for-one match for Arthur Morgan's iconic hat. The seller even mutters that she does not know where it came from. You can buy it for just a few bucks, five dollars as Jason or thirty-five as Lucia, and it stays permanently unlocked in your wardrobe. If the yard sale is empty when you arrive, just leave the block and return another day to trigger it.

Rockstar did not stop at the hat, there is an even wilder Arthur Morgan tribute hiding in South Leonard County. Head over to Valentine Drive, yes, literally named after Valentine, and walk into the Wild West mini golf park called Frontier Adventure Golf. Stroll along the wooden saloon boardwalk and look down by your boots. Trotting around the course is a tiny Chihuahua wearing a miniature cowboy figurine strapped to its saddle, complete with Arthur's brown hat, blue shirt, and a tiny revolver waving in the air. Even better, Jason can crouch down and pet the Ride 'Em Chihuahua while mini Arthur keeps patrolling the boardwalk. And for hardcore Red Dead lore hunters, check Brian's marina for a paperback book detailing Flaco Hernandez's death at Cairn Lake, name-dropping Blackwater, Armadillo, and New Austin, plus rare Leonida cars sporting New Austin license plates.

Finally, because Vice City is Rockstar's Miami, they packed in a four-part tribute to Showtime's Dexter Morgan, and most players only find part one. Head to Washington Island in Vice Beach and look for Trinity Apartments, a direct nod to Season Four's Trinity Killer. Climb the spiral staircase to the second-floor northwest corner unit to see Dexter's exact walkway, pool courtyard, and the outdoor AC unit where he hid his blood slides. Now climb the side ladder up onto the roof beside the dome, sitting on the concrete is Dexter's full kill kit: a syringe, rope, purple tape, gloves, a free knife you can equip, and sixty-eight dollars inside the toolbox. Walk down to the dock right outside to spot his twin-engine boat, the Slice of Life. Then drive down to Surfside Drive in Key Lento, pop a locked garage door, and you will uncover a full plastic-wrapped kill room lined with cleavers and freezers, plus human bones scattered on the ocean floor nearby.
