In November 2022, NASA's new Moon rocket stood on Launch Pad 39B, ready for its first flight. The Space Launch System lit up the night sky over Florida. Ninety minutes later, the upper stage sent the Orion spacecraft towards the Moon. Five days out, Orion skimmed just 130 kilometres above the lunar surface. Then it climbed into a huge, distant orbit, and on the twenty-eighth of November it was 432,210 kilometres from Earth, farther than any spacecraft built for astronauts had ever gone. From there, its cameras looked back at the Earth and the Moon together. On the fifth of December Orion swung past the Moon one last time and headed home. Six days later, three parachutes lowered Orion into the Pacific, off Baja California.
