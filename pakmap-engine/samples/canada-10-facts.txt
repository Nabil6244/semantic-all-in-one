Here are ten facts about Canada that most people never learn. Fact one. Canada holds about sixty percent of all the lakes on Earth. Most of them have never been given a name. Fact two. It is the second largest country in the world, nearly ten million square kilometres of land and water. Fact three. Canada has the longest coastline of any country, far longer than Norway or Indonesia. Fact four. For all that space, Canada is one of the emptiest places anywhere. Four people for every square kilometre, while Bangladesh has more than a thousand. Fact five. Vancouver is one of the wettest big cities in the country. Rain falls almost every month, and the autumn is the wettest of all. Fact six. The population has tripled since nineteen fifty, from fourteen million to more than forty million people. Fact seven. Five provinces and territories are larger than most countries. Nunavut alone is bigger than Mexico. Fact eight. Nunavut is also the least crowded place of its size, home to about forty thousand people. Fact nine. About nine in ten Canadians live within a hundred miles of the border with the United States. Fact ten. Canada crosses six time zones, from Newfoundland all the way to the Yukon.
