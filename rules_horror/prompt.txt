The video is flat illustrated scenes whose colour follows the story (night, warm, cold, red for scares), with one red accent. LENGTH AND VOICE
- 900 to 1,150 spoken words (about 6 to 7 minutes at 165 words a minute). Second person ("you"). Short sentences. Plain words. No sound-effect words, no stage directions.

STORY SHAPE (follow it in this order)
1. HOOK: "If you ever [see or hear X], do [Y]. Do not [A]. Do not [B]." One or two sentences.
2. NARROWING: "I am not talking about [the normal thing]. I am talking about [the wrong one]."
3. TURN: the one detail that makes it wrong.
4. PROMISE: "Follow these seven rules," and plant one line such as "most people break the third one". Rule 1 must start between 30 and 80 seconds in.
5. SEVEN RULES. Each rule is a command, then the reason, then a scare or someone who broke it. Say each rule as its own short sentence ("Do not turn on the lights.") so it can be shown on a card.
6. TWIST: from rule 7 on, 17 to 37 percent of all the words. Begin with something like "There is one thing I have not told you yet."
7. CLOSING LINE: repeat the opening ("So if you ever ..."), then end on one last scare.
