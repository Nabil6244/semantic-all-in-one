If you ever wake up at three in the morning, and see a deer standing in your yard, do not wave. Do not call out. I am not talking about a normal deer. I am talking about one that is far too tall, with legs that bend in the wrong places. And it is not looking at you. It is looking at something standing behind you. Follow these three rules and you might see the sun come up. Do not turn on the lights. Whatever is behind you wants you to look. Keep your eyes on the window. Do not count its legs. A man down the street counted them. He got to nine. Do not answer the door. It knocks with a voice you know. So if you ever see a tall deer in your yard, stay in bed, and keep the lights off.
